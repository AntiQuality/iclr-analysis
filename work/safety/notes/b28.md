# b28 notes (173 papers)

- **New threats in the 2027 agent supply chain.** 2027 papers go beyond classic prompt injection. They cover installable skills and SKILL.md packages (TTUWMVX6S7, TrustProbe source-to-sink fuzzing), audits of persistent skill writes (aED3Fh5eVk), and privacy leaks through shared agent memory and KV caches (e8IOFEj7Kh, which includes an openclaw pilot and audits of shipped workspace assistants; hwFK3ka2BR, LCGuard for latent KV communication). They also cover cross-session poisoning of shared state in enterprise multi-agent systems (kPII2EfBf7, ErosionBench, labelled against OWASP LLM Top-10) and "branch steering" against Dual-LLM computer-use agents (BReqQMFlJb, STEER-Bench). The 2026 side of this batch has nothing comparable.
- **Agent evaluation now looks at authorization, not task success.** kuSAqpyELw replays AgentDojo trajectories to check intent-state consistency, meaning unauthorized side effects. u8IrCiuCqe shows that jailbreak context lingers in tool agents and finds a "late-commit" layer pattern. r3Q2dkT0Ok combines 98 languages, 8K/32K context and five named guards (WildGuard, GuardReasoner, etc.) to measure guardrail bypass in tool agents. AgentDojo and AEGIS2.0 recur as substrates.
- **AI control and CoT monitoring become a 2027 cluster.** vXekakDqvv argues that CoT monitoring can fail even when CoT is necessary and proposes a hierarchy of necessity levels. Rf01ZgPB3q (TAEA) hardens code monitors against attacker-written text with a typed evidence boundary. fNhnH7qAaW bounds power-seeking and shutdown resistance through risk-limiting multi-agent orchestration. TcLLmoNoKI uses natural-language autoencoders and activation verbalizers to audit concealment and injection. In 2026, the nearest work is LH-Deception (2r10vpYiti), a behavioural study of long-horizon deception.
- **Methods shift: interpretability is used as a lever on refusal and safety.** Examples are SAE-supervised safety alignment that cuts over-refusal (Ft0PKiQS9d), safety subspaces that are under 5% of dimensions and transfer across 30 models (bMMpt9SFy6), fold geometry at refuse/comply boundaries (8BXlj4TyR1), cross-modal "safety translation bottleneck" tracing in VLMs (r80YG5Iw5O), and causal-fragility analysis to improve GCG/AutoDAN transfer (p1Zxt5QWZe). 2026 counterparts are cruder: steering for audio LLMs (pz3WCd3HtG) and momentum steering (ETT804iVAt).
- **Reward hacking moves into rubric-based and multimodal RL.** ImqXHecKq3 (Rubric Dropout; the training judge and the OOD judge diverge under GRPO), s57tr9JibG (visually-bypassed shortcuts in MLLM RL) and 5hx4qZB8H8 (editing the reward-model head to remove length/confidence bias) are all 2027. Honesty and abstention via RL appear in both years: ZjidJlTOxd in 2026, SdNqYzEWkM (detection-to-abstention gap in reasoning models) in 2027.
- **New attack surfaces in jailbreak work.** Targets now include diffusion VLMs (BoKNgD7p47, GPO-V attacks the denoising trajectory), audio LLMs through codec latents that survive Opus/MP3 compression (H0haErGk5A), and multi-turn text-vision jailbreaks (h0lOaeDwF2, 2026). Dual-use cyber appears with DoGNAVY-Exploit (mHbJHNVC2w), a harness that lets open-weight agents do multi-stage exploitation.
- **2027 privacy and provenance papers are more measurement-heavy.** Examples are prompt-authorship re-identification on SWE-Chat and WildChat (XBHclQOwQi), visual canaries for memorization in document VLMs (Ta2WxQVUhI), quantization selectively erasing memorized text (fDCtw2lywD), embedding inversion of Nemotron-3-Embed (zJD5hkamGW), whether distillation sources stay detectable (SMR34sF88u), and proof-of-training "witnesses" (CHXRu3nqg0). Detection now covers human-AI collaborative text (HXok21UQfd, C-HAT-Bench in Chinese) and multi-bit watermarks for bit-wise image generators (yrfvyxmuVH).
- **Embodied and physical safety benchmarks grow in 2027.** Examples are injury-narrative physical-hazard benchmarks (go44bcifFA), EmbodiedSafe runtime hazard handling (c4xVSwQcrD), information-theoretic VLA failure detection (KVNlfq7GAO), repair of LLM robot commands (9VuHwpuwqT) and AV sensor-spoofing detection (iVlKaIVWn9). About 20% of the batch is rel=0, mostly reward or RL papers for image restoration and generation and pure interpretability theory.
