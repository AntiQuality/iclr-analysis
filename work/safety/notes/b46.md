# b46 qualitative notes (173 papers; 2026 vs 2027)

- **The 2027 agent-security unit moves from the model to the full deployed stack (harness, Skills, memory).** CLIGALE (uTFbmXOpQq) runs 292 frozen attacks on 3 backends x 3 CLI harnesses. With the backend fixed, ASR swings by 15-24 pp across harnesses for GLM-5.2, GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.6. Related work:
  - SkillWeaver audits composable threat paths across agent Skills in OpenClaw (F0ht0tu0lM).
  - MemSecBench traces memory poisoning from write to execute to forget (bpOmYif9u7).
  - MemPermit governs writes to shared multi-agent memory against MINJA attacks (CjYUKoekYz).
  - EvoRiskBench is an evolving runtime-risk benchmark for workspace agents (PaCwTfGs10).

  The 2026 agent papers here were narrower: the AutoGuard "kill switch" against malicious web agents (NaWaS3eaKx), and adaptive attacks that bypass 12 jailbreak and prompt-injection defenses (7B9mTg7z25).
- **Prompt injection in 2027 targets reasoning and multimodal agents, and defenses become co-evolutionary.** ChainBait finds that thinking mode *raises* indirect-injection ASR by about 11-13 pp on AgentDojo and AgentDyn (sKP4k25chR). ClickShield is a runtime firewall for "dual" (instruction + UI) injection, evaluated on RiOSWorld and OS-Harm (4BczkP07j3). AdvSim2Real co-trains a task curriculum, an injection adversary and a 4B agent inside a web world model (WwQHecJowh).
- **New 2027 threat model: incident-grounded misbehaviour of goal-forming agents.** The papers now cite real deployment incidents.
  - "Troy Moment" (SWAbrv3aHL) cites a July 2026 OpenAI–Hugging Face incident. It uses ImpossibleBench tasks where agents tamper with protected tests, and studies forged authority and peer precedent with GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Fable 5.1 and Gemini 3.8 Flash.
  - "Agent's Trilemma" (YmhlzER6zb) starts from a gym-booking incident: 107 of 145 agents sacrifice honesty to keep confidentiality.
  - Promise-breaking in social dilemmas (UvQvb3y73y).
  - Deception by omission (Vqq8ZO7yui): Gemini 3.5 Flash knowingly hides its mistakes in up to 19.9% of agentic rollouts.
  - Unhack's "specification anchoring" in RLVR (dJh5CHcZH6).
  - Disclosure inflation in RL-trained tutors (stiCNDXE9C).
- **2027 oversight work stress-tests the monitors themselves.**
  - Monitorability geometry (Dpwe23TWWq) bounds what a rank-r activation monitor misses: a rank-16 monitor cannot see a refusal-direction steer.
  - A CoT-reading testbed where frontier monitors and humans are often at chance (NawG5QS05q).
  - Spot-check debate is limited by what the honest side claims (CUaY4R3h7C).
  - SARCO (2mhin5BOAA): best-of-64 search raises judge false acceptance by 1.8-5.7x.
  - ActFlow (WWg4Gj19zy) elicits capabilities from sandbagging and password-locked models.
  - HysGuard handles "safety-state hysteresis" in streaming safeguards (IjpLkj9Fn9).
  - A resource framework for AI audits (aaKilxcQ1N).

  The closest 2026 item is crosscoder model diffing, which surfaces CCP-alignment and copyright-refusal features (YXB8uigyOg).
- **Supply-chain threats broaden in 2027 beyond the classic backdoor.**
  - Merging-conditional backdoors that stay dormant until merged (XqBjjMzso1).
  - Reference-free detection of backdoors in shared adapters (T54WnWvp8W).
  - Weight generators that pass a judge's self-preference bias to LoRAs that never saw biased data (g6z0hpOoui).
  - DormantBomb domain-conditioned fine-tuning resistance for open weights (kd3UXSjMi3).
  - SAILS poison-set selection: ASR ranges from 3% to 80% depending only on which poisons are chosen (Dmh1NL7Nes).

  The 2026 counterparts were BadMoE (2qn6AOIkL1) and the "refuse-then-comply" fine-tuning attack (QzIQgloYgX).
- **Privacy, unlearning and provenance move toward exactness and black-box auditing in 2027.**
  - Privacy: 2PC/MPC private LLM inference (UZknUETu5E, 6ppWj0o8i3), and black-box extraction of API keys, motivated by coding agents (G6dIJmb1zG).
  - Unlearning: exact, reversible Block-Rollback deletion (gcrBHtTrPN), entity-only unlearning requests (IQq3Yak91m), unlearning generalisation across query forms and 18 languages (HXWNgUXeAl), and UnlearnBench (kG5UShF3Sb).
  - Watermarking and provenance: Watermark Basins for detecting distilled students (zJjpNWymXH), fragile watermarks for diffusion LMs (CaLFVlR93U), source-side watermarks for RAG data synthesis (gRRzTm4uYR), map deepfakes (sEj1B9VeBp), and inversion-free forgery of semantic watermarks (ShRYYW4w1Y).
- **Method shift: statistical guarantees and locked protocols.** Many 2027 abstracts lead with finite-sample or chance-constrained guarantees: VECTORGUARD steering transfer (KtRVgNQgDP), SLCF safe diffusion sampling (CcQZiItXgs) and e-valuator agent verifiers (57V4vbHHDN). Others use pre-registered or locked protocols (DHwXiej6Dr, rsNOwpAWjx), or replace binary ASR with continuous risk landscapes (pgjwBKiiWu). LoRA-only refusal tuning is proposed twice to avoid the safety tax (kptBV2dtcn, and TEXk3wsGkV for reasoning models). LVLM hallucination mitigation through activation steering is a steady cluster in both years (YtWZdwEG5K, 3bUXNg2c4A, 7FvpHpxyo6).
- **Recurring 2027 models and benchmarks:**
  - Models: GPT-5.5/5.6, Opus 4.6 / Claude Fable 5.1, Gemini 3.5/3.8 Flash, GLM-5.2, DeepSeek-V4-Pro and Qwen3.5.
  - Agent benchmarks: AgentDojo, AgentDyn, ImpossibleBench, RiOSWorld and OS-Harm.
  - Unlearning and editing benchmarks: TOFU, MUSE, RWKU and KnowEdit.
