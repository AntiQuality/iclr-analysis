# b11 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent threat models move from prompt text to infrastructure and lifecycle.** The 2026 agent papers are mostly attack chains or benchmarks (STAC tool-chaining jailbreak 8nTMKYHkrV, BackBench harm recovery qUeL7HwLDa). The 2027 papers attack or defend the agent stack itself. Examples: memory-injection lifecycle red teaming (rKF6C9GfjZ), graph-based tool-call authorization against "policy laundering" (MlJhZy1MZM, GraphPermit), action-backtracking RL against indirect prompt injection (psmPjQmrZ4, SecStep), GEO web-evidence poisoning of deep-search agents (8EmiXfV800, HAE-GEO) and stale-memory state arbitration (Wne2AYfvqG).
- **Production coding agents are now named red-team targets.** IKUjfAknOl (AHA) runs a Karpathy-style "autoresearch" loop against Claude Code and Codex and keeps falsifiable vulnerability concepts, not individual attack strings. RedHerring (qMOCg7qXrq) flips the cyber threat model: it plants certifiable decoys so that LLM vulnerability-hunting agents waste their verification budget.
- **Judges and verifiers as an attack surface is a new 2027 cluster.** Several papers show that text around a decision moves the verdict. In hBwEvj0bJo, execution traces make video-agent MLLM judges accept 78–90% of failed clips. In 8qnklea4uX, compliance claims cut verification audits. Other 2027 papers on this theme: 0xSVxgdfHH (judge confidence as a reward-hacking signal) and BKIwMYZPx7 (evidence-first verifier, Sauron). In 2026 the same area was about classic reward over-optimisation and length bias (hKxYESOzen).
- **Scheming and collusion research matures into multi-agent and statistical work.** 2026 has DeceptionBench with CoT self-monitoring (lrCVJmOgAP) and metacognition tests (gb9HR8hxtU). 2027 adds:
  - collusion initiation through strategic vagueness (O5wP85WFVL);
  - multi-agent collusion detection with activation probes (zNolMfwGC7, NARCBench);
  - a Neyman–Pearson limit on certifying evaluation-aware agents (CxqMrLiKg2);
  - debate honesty and evidence disclosure (Ei7EGvZlMz).
- **Interpretability has become an engineering tool for safety.** 2027 examples:
  - refusal-circuit weight edits (xaOXC9sahX);
  - Reward Override Components showing RLVR (RL with verifiable rewards) takes control away from refusal (N4kL8TnIRh);
  - the cost that steering imposes on linear monitors (0Ak6YfrqMe);
  - steering-vector channels against prompt injection (slkIMkRq1L);
  - few-weight behaviour modulation (IuMamxh76q).

  Subliminal and distillation transfer is a recurring 2027 theme: homeopathic dilution of poisoning (DhZoJjdjzK), on-policy distillation spreading backdoors (j9uWANGL9k), and censorship vs. lie-rate dissociation (j46QkEcSt1).
- **New model families bring new attack surfaces.** These are backdoors in diffusion language models (attack BadDLM skoBAkjFAr, defense UNMASK PsrmEN6HHg), prefilling jailbreaks on discrete-token speech LLMs (q1Zt6mK0Kg), cost-amplification suffixes on adaptive reasoning models (3z0Fu8sf5R), and patch-end attacks on time-series foundation models (Z5IUN9uqfT).
- **Evaluation methodology gets more rigorous in 2027.** Papers use pre-registration, certificates and reliability audits. Examples: a jailbreak-defense certificate separating prevention from delay (nA2Ms41Mqs), audited judge reliability for mental-health agents (0e3DjzUsCa), and schema laundering in embodied safety gates (9pOI1s0VET). Domain safety benchmarks are spreading to finance (DXxip7cMa7), aviation (6bT67VKN6x), crisis support (vhVUssaO4u) and functional visual harm in image generators (K5tNDhKqB3, FVH-Bench).
- **Recurring models and names.** Qwen2.5/Qwen3 (and "Qwen3.6/3.8") and Llama-3.1-8B are the default open models. 2027 abstracts cite GPT-5.4-mini, GPT-5.5, GPT-5.6, Claude Opus 5 and gpt-oss-20B. Gemma-2/3 serve as SAE substrates. Watermarking and deepfake work is steady across both years and shifts toward MLLM-based, explainable forensics (Fa1wVk3poL, IBHE5Nj61C) and forgery attacks (zEAaLYe02Q).
