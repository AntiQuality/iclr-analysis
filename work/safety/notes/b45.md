# b45 qualitative notes (173 papers; 2026 vs 2027)

- **The 2027 agent attack surface moves into the harness, memory and skills layer.** Examples: ELLA, where benign visual evidence hijacks a self-evolving agent's own memory writer (A1R0FUZrnQ); SkillDRE, which evolves malicious skill packages to evade both pre-execution scanners and runtime defenses (XGBvOkQIzf); implicit-tool attacks, where coding agents are led to configure benign extensions that later perform sensitive operations (80% success vs 0.2% for direct requests; ZcOCE4B0Aw); HarnessSecGym, built from 313 real harness CVE-style records (TToNLahVXD); and DefenseFlywheel, which co-evolves attacks and harness-level patches on AgentDojo Banking (6gD5WHS4ei). The 2026 agent papers in this batch were general benchmarks and alignment data: OASIS on intent concealment (0TNomhjCCi) and AgentAlign (DdHrylM8Tr).
- **Attacks now target the safety stack itself (2027).** Examples: ProbeFool evades activation-probe jailbreak monitors through attention routing (ivxKlg0hXC); "Twenty-five samples" poisons self-adapting guardrails that retrain on weakly labelled traffic (bLGeIyOpEo); adaptive attacks break near-perfect prompt-injection detectors, with Unicode/full-width tricks reaching 96% bypass (4LlPvx9fmD); and test-gated judge updates are shown to be "green because they leaked", with the authors retracting their own earlier result (ajQKOrbHBk).
- **Multi-agent social failure modes are a growing 2027 cluster.** Examples: deceiver *proportion* rather than group size drives defection (v0ntzSNixA); homogenization, polarization and inertia appear in code, hiring and peer-review MAS (giMpNTtGsL); "factionalism", where exposing model-family labels splits cooperation (O8ZotTjxRL); and peer-reliability learning against sycophantic conformity (dj41CyOSnJ). In 2026, multi-agent work here was mostly representational similarity and cooperation (MHA8oTxoei).
- **Fine-tuning and alignment-robustness work becomes more mechanistic and measurement-focused in 2027.** Examples: SafeOff shows that benign rewrites teaching a refusal-to-compliance shift erode safety (ly2a5AxXAH); a stage-resolved study of safety removal decouples attack success from utility loss (srB3lzJVqR); "un-alignment" is used as an audit, finding that harm recognition and refusal directions are decoupled in LVLMs (RymkoKMLCB); RefusalTrace localizes refusal into encode/integrate/execute SAE feature sets (NxX49DNW2C); refusal failures under tool schemas reflect action rather than recognition (6OAEXc8Amg); and constitutional adapters distil a constitution into LoRA or steering objects (kX23kRoFcf). 2026 counterparts were evaluation-awareness measurement (izqDX2cBJ5), CoT monitorability (qWmsebKO14) and iterated-RLHF overoptimisation (efoe5VJfKz).
- **Unlearning evaluation keeps moving toward recovery and leakage.** In 2026: leak@k under sampling (rzi77zNngG), Prileak relearning (DXqiaXrNLu) and CIR robust hazardous-knowledge unlearning on WMDP (QSxnB2PXJb). In 2027: reasoning-time recovery of unlearned answers on TOFU (KL95EGhmuB), verifiable forgetting in agent long-term memory (LeWrK9dkR6), revocable memory cartridges for RTL LLMs (Rx63MyqDyn), and inference-time steering for code unlearning (Lv6kI6mc5P).
- **Reward hacking and oversight: there is a new 2027 focus on *implicit* hacking.** MILE detects reward hacking that is invisible in the CoT, using answer likelihood under truncated reasoning and building on TRACE (FV0WWmGABO). Debate is shown to be compromised by rhetorical and cognitive-bias strategies (ANGZvuNeSv), and RLHF rationality is made context-dependent to handle annotator bias (DxLKWwiiOP). The "preference trap" covers persuasion agents that exploit user simulators (YhKqfjMUBh).
- **Diffusion language models appear as a new safety target in both years.** Examples: priming jailbreaks via affirmative tokens at intermediate denoising steps (2026, ZMzha5gbnF) and multi-bit watermarking for LLaDA/Dream dLLMs (2027, Uh2vvMhjmA). Privacy work in 2027 leans heavily toward cryptographic private inference (HE/MPC: KA04FSmk4k, IP1H2coORG, b0YQ6hV6y1, EpEsHRuArF) and model inversion from released VLM LoRA adapters (wBAFJIrEpM).
- **Recurring artefacts and method style.** AgentDojo, τ-bench, HarmBench, TOFU, MUSE, WMDP and PKU-SafeRLHF recur. 2027 models include Qwen3/Qwen3.6-27B, Llama-3.x, Gemma-2 and FLUX.2 (the editor targeted by IRIS anti-edit, suNcQvUkiN). As in other batches, 2027 abstracts emphasise controls, negative results and retractions: hiring-audit instrument variance under NYC LL144 (ZL85LzxhGG), pitfalls in measuring data-pruning class damage (2LdyUr6wUw), and CKA blind spots in RLVR (fLlxjKVVqi). AI-generated image and forgery detection remains a large, steady cluster in both years, with a 2027 shift toward continual/test-time adaptation (5jWlgkiP05, 9KtcjE2y2l).
