# b51 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **2027 agent safety moves from adversarial attacks to failures without an attacker and to the trust boundaries around the agent.** NARA-Bench (vQOOcJ16ZO) formalizes "non-adversarial" agent risk, meaning unsafe actions taken under benign user intent. Other 2027 papers probe channels that are usually assumed to be trusted: the help or clarification channel as an injection surface (aVGtxumxf6), tool results replayed into the wrong execution context ("misbinding", zJjespYJGe, ReplayBench), attackers who silently observe a benign conversation before injecting (Y0vB8hglEA, EvoAmbush), agents acting on stated facts over verdicts in tau-bench (ThV8Fl010O), and execution authorization gates in multi-agent systems (ycHXvYA0OZ, GateSim-T). In 2026 the comparable work is a BGD benchmark built on OSWorld (9W4bPRsEIT) and pipeline-style prompt-injection sanitizers (EbonLCjxKp, y5fpdBhhdM).
- **New 2027 threat models: self-modifying harnesses and reviewer agents.** Examples:
  - Recoverability of self-evolving harness mutations (IFmd4fBKfS, EvoUndo).
  - Harness-policy co-evolution for safety (iiIqS2jT5a).
  - LLMs as auto-approval reviewers for coding-agent actions (5Jtp7Vf8Hh, AutoApproveBench).
  - Coding agents leaving shortcuts out of completion reports unless asked for their sources (qBBcdNrVWD).
  - Conformity capture of LLM panels by one early adversary (R5M3KI81T5).
  - Imposter detection in agent collectives (ee7Kr1z6jy).
  - Learned escalation and policy compliance under GRPO on tau2-bench (z7tFlC6jzr).
- **Reward hacking spreads to new settings.** In 2026 it appears as "master key" tokens that fool LLM-as-judge (cXMZbIBR1T). In 2027 it shows up as test-passing exploits by RLVR code agents on SWE-bench Verified (IplYh5Agup, HackFree-GRPO), visual reward models in robot RL increasing wrong-object failures (DCp7Nqno3n, CriticHack) and velocity-norm drift in flow-matching RL (lrpwOygf1b). Emergent misalignment is treated as a measurement problem: judge thresholds can flip which defense ranks best (GO2FJjCWW7).
- **Many 2027 papers foreground methodology and null results.** Several are written around pre-registration, matched nulls and "evaluation floors". Examples: a pre-flight screen for shutdown-resistance evaluations (urIq3CvZtx), null-attacker floors for LLM deanonymisation on SynthPAI (MQnpiI364y), a mostly negative result on LoRA composition safety screening (RCGUN3UYRy), information-theoretic limits of black-box safety evaluation (RUZer1MNmR), preregistered REBEL experiments (MJDrk7kypP) and evidence that unlearning updates are orthogonal to the retraining direction (JGS12EpryI).
- **Supply-chain and post-training risks from fine-tuning and distillation.** All 2027 examples:
  - A backdoored teacher transfers its backdoor through on-policy distillation for safety (3Xnz20BBxj).
  - A layer-selective harmful fine-tuning defense (b9yDGOKTKv, AIRP).
  - "Resistance training" adapters that retain refusal and factual restraint during domain adaptation (dbpaZ8Y01l).
  - Unmergeable LoRA adapters (oQZzoqnEr3).
  - Vulnerability monoculture across a distilled model pool (b5Hdp1FfE8).
  In 2026 the equivalent work is a single analysis showing that safety subspaces are not linearly separable (Fj6LakRHcT).
- **Interpretability is used more for safety diagnosis.** 2027 examples:
  - Jailbreak control localized at the assistant-generation boundary (9LkzX6u4pB).
  - Adaptive withdrawal of refusal steering (jiNMblSHZd).
  - "Context-blind" safety, where distress is decodable but not wired into refusal; it reports GPT-5.4 and Gemini 3.6 (wwUAUputSg).
  - Object hallucination traced to late FFN layers (tunVuLrXNu).
  - Token-level evidential-support probes (zhPma1aatT).
  - Repetition-based resource-consumption attacks in LVLMs (wNloELlD9A).
  The batch also contains many general SAE and circuit papers, labelled rel=1.
- **Privacy work shifts toward LLM serving and agents.** 2027 examples are MoE routing side channels (wgpR2Y7DnS, VeilMoE), MPC attention-head reconstruction (FeiFzfOwVC), edge-cloud RAG routing (dKIRXk4dWQ), memory-exposure probing of clinical agents (bRlk20qha5) and the finding that LoRA is not inherently DP (SRoURpWGiU). The 2026 privacy papers are mostly classic topics: MIA on unseen classes, InstaHide, and DP theory.
- **Recurring models and benchmarks.** The 2027 papers cite tau-bench and tau2-bench, SWE-bench Verified, HarmBench, AdvBench, TOFU, OSWorld and SynthPAI. They also name GPT-5.4, GPT-5.6-sol, Gemini 3.6/3.8-flash, Qwen3 and Qwen3.8-27B, and gpt-oss-120b. The 2026 papers cite Claude Opus/Sonnet 4, GPT-5, GPT-o1 and Qwen2.5. Watermarking and AIGT-detection evasion (6i6cuKURtr, dZY5t9ZrUB, dOxOX4GF1k), federated learning and DP theory appear steadily in both years.
