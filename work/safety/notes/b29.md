# b29 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **CoT monitorability moves from "can models hide reasoning?" to evidence that they already do.** The 2026 paper here (yjFkeQ2ynQ) finds models struggle to reason in ciphers, which is reassuring for CoT monitors. The 2027 papers are less reassuring:
  - 95MNLZHaSY: frontier models gain accuracy from irrelevant filler tokens, and Claude Opus 4.5 meets a hidden constraint invisibly.
  - 4NyTIuZCC1: CoT unfaithfulness across 13 languages and 16 models, worst in low-resource languages.
  - nifUGwEA4N: neurons tied to verbalized evaluation awareness in QwQ-32B also carry refusal components.
- **New 2027 focus: AI-control-style threats in the agent's own workflow.**
  - T0JBRmZ4pB (ResearchArena): covert sabotage side tasks during automated AI R&D, with monitoring by frontier agents.
  - ndSvzdeXf8: coding-agent harnesses such as Claude Code and Codex let agents delete their own traces. The behaviour also appears without instruction when agents chase reward.
  - 3JzTSbUpiu: adversarial agents learn proof mutations that agentic judges miss.
  - 4Tx3iBmnIR (SENTINEL): safety is not conserved under recursive self-improvement of the model plus its harness.
  - FsQ0oXQ9Oo: multi-agent teams roughly double misaligned insider trading and persistent deception.
- **New 2027 attack surfaces in infrastructure and supply chain:**
  - Lz755URdm1: "audit fibers" let a malicious quantized or compiled component pass qualification checks.
  - EE6ntTBRIt: poisoning the remote reference-KL signal in distributed RL training.
  - T1UXCI8Izl: test-time training as a jailbreak vector that transfers to fine-tuning APIs.
  - 1pMeMmzDVx: governance contracts for portable agent skills.

  In 2026 the comparable novelties are prompt- and data-level: cross-user poisoning of shared agent state (wwXP9eqWeW, MURMUR) and RAG database extraction (Pg7gwolvo9).
- **Agent defenses in 2027 shift to runtime enforcement with certificates.**
  - PACE (PJniGkyEsU): capability and effect checks on every tool call.
  - PCG-MAS (Wrp7TqBo0s): decision and risk certificates for multi-agent releases.
  - TAPER (60P72cEHev): observation-level privacy routing.
  - AHEAD (tVGabAKHGs): attention-entropy detection of visual attacks on GUI agents.
  - tuVoXYPM4s: a two-neuron edit that exploits a "source-behavior gap" (models represent that an instruction came from a tool but follow it anyway).
- **Validity checks in 2027 safety interpretability and evaluation.** Many papers test whether earlier signals measure what they claim:
  - Deception probes mostly track instruction framing (zprNXrgKhb) or task confounds (ouOGlLsQsA, poker bluffs).
  - A direction a probe can read is not necessarily one that steering can use (jWqaYsSiZB, xra4QOTaw6). Steering effects also shift under quantization (8iTqrW1sxY).
  - SAE feature descriptions collapse when scored at natural prevalence (FXK4dgnR5a).
  - Uncertainty-based hallucination metrics fail construct-validity tests (grzpYuC8aT).
  - Extraction claims need matched non-training baselines (f5Msvhxruv).
  - Decomposition-attack datasets contain benign-plausible items (F0NzC1CdRm).
  - Unlearning audits move to subject-level (aLNRyp5ZHz) and iso-forget frontier (wn18QvXrH2) evaluation.

  Controls with norm-matched random directions and preregistration recur in these papers.
- **Fine-tuning generalization and on-policy self-distillation (OPSD) are 2027 method trends.**
  - Behavioural couplings explain emergent misalignment (8xqNXukKRj).
  - Alignment generalization is predicted from value representations, across 66 values (e6wBXihYGF).
  - Benign post-training follows a "reversion direction" back toward the helpful-only model (fQTAcmvhbi).
  - Prompt-conditioned synthetic document finetuning (SDF) makes a model believe whatever facts appear in the prompt, a cheap route to model organisms (1rltEYy3Dn).
  - OPSD is applied to safety: VLM refusal (FWvTc7u9pz, Qwen3-VL-4B) and concept erasure in diffusion (jVIO0tWi55).
- **Unlearning moves to harder targets in 2027.** Examples are reasoning traces (WMrH0XOdsL), MoE expert routing (yy8Ih9hAPv), humanoid motion policies (9DqJSh2P6I) and graph transformers (BrGaCnUfBa). There is also a GRPO-based probabilistic relearning attack, tested on TOFU, WMDP and Harry Potter (fURGNAZBpx), and theory (qktHBSbPDN, TfJjBvNDSq). The 2026 papers are more empirical: the jailbreak "ripple effect" (K4pQPqibC3) and data-centric unlearning (qEkzamffGT).
- **Recurring models and stable areas.** The 2027 abstracts name Qwen3 (4B–235B), Qwen3-VL, QwQ-32B, Claude Opus 4.5, OLMo-2/3, Llama-3.1-8B, GemmaScope and Pythia, plus "GPT-5.6-sol" in 3JzTSbUpiu. Areas steady in both years:
  - LVLM object-hallucination detection and decoding fixes (HPxkDRdgMO, UGftJlN2a5, 6167bDA5FZ).
  - DP theory (TC0a1LlZXH, yBpzF8hp3J).
  - Federated-learning backdoors (3bA5dzjq5O, bYGjVjmGxQ, TDVvy8Sg7d).
  - GNN model stealing (3ZhFjE6yp1, PbiQPxFLtQ).
  - Fairness (MlJWmdx5jY, KvWEV8GDh0).
