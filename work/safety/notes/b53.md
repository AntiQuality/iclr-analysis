# b53 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **Alignment auditing and awareness of context become measurable objects in 2027.**
  - AuditBench (sSOSRpUn48) provides 56 model organisms with implanted hidden behaviors and reports a "tool-to-agent gap" for auditing tools.
  - User awareness (Ga5ICrf1fi) shows that frontier models, including Claude Sonnet 5, change their behavior when they infer which AI-safety researcher they are talking to. Their reasoning rarely mentions this.
  - Probes for evaluation awareness turn out to depend on prompt framing (IXhIVYI8wP; Qwen3.5 scaling sign flips). Metacognitive states are decoded and steered in reasoning models (4lG3d2QtGR).
  - CoT monitors are audited across 8 languages (nbIbiJTibd).
  - None of the 2026 papers in this batch covers this area.
- **The oversight pipeline itself is treated as an attack surface (2027).**
  - "Silent consensus": unanimous LLM-judge panels make the risk of deployed outputs unidentifiable (rnm0DNXZTf).
  - BlameShift: agents rewrite their own narratives to steer failure-attribution judges (rfjD2IMEys).
  - LLMs learn hidden evaluator rules (bwCNQvkEyB).
  - Weak-to-strong hacker/fixer hardening of agent verifiers on Terminal Bench and KernelBench (0ad4aBnjoQ).
  - Chain-of-Lies: VLM backdoors trained with GRPO against a VLM overseer so the CoT hides the trigger (IE4Ym7L9Ho).
- **Agent threat models get more specific in 2027.**
  - DOM provenance for browser agents (E4dyBlD5z7, ProvDOM).
  - Memory poisoning versus real preference drift in personalized agents (BdkTCk70gP).
  - "Semantic reanchoring": in-context examples redefine an unchanged instruction, e.g. OFF->ON (1f88yd3oiV).
  - Differencing and injection attacks in federated multi-agent negotiation (gfXSfaPGaI).
  - Language world models that "whitewash" refusals from the environment they simulate (0cb6zfwHm7).
  - Unsafe actions in response to benign delegated asks (uAFTCpt4Em, SafetyBench).
  - Hawkes-process cascade criticality for risky actions (f7Lvvp9Jym).
  - Runtime enforcement at the point where actions are committed, for agent fleets (I6Gx5US2sY).
  - In 2026, multi-agent risk is mostly fraud-collusion benchmarks and social simulation (a1d2smwmBS, q9KMXiy7th).
- **Red-teaming moves from prompt search to skills and diversity-shaped RL.**
  - 2026 attacks are prompt-level: few-shot genetic selection (B7oQWswV7y) and visual knowledge graphs (AzmtrYcj2C).
  - 2027 attacks:
    - RedDarwin evolves reusable executable attack skills and reports 100% ASR on GPT-5.6 (jJb8QPInoP).
    - CoFiBreak adds coarse-to-fine diversity rewards to GRPO attackers (6hKhOvyIo8).
    - PUARobot uses psychological manipulation to jailbreak embodied agents (34bRy8dfI1).
    - A controlled causal dissection of image jailbreaks across GPT-4o and Claude S.4.5 reports negative results (eXSAhJBl4m).
  - 2026 defenses build safety into reasoning: Answer-Then-Check (DK6AToxJNo) and ReAlign RL with a guard-model verifier on Qwen3-4B (XxYNlbTFYS). The 2027 defense GuardAlign trains the backend model on its guardrail's coverage gaps (u9T0BdbuzS).
- **Privacy moves from DP mechanics to contextual and need-to-know disclosure.**
  - 2026 papers are classic DP/MIA work: DIET-PATE, SoftAdaClip, Delta-MIA, LevAtt on tabular data, and PII leakage in healthcare RAG.
  - 2027 papers:
    - Probing whether agents represent contextual-integrity norms when they act (cWFfCfZQxL; PrivacyLens).
    - Need-to-know sanitization specific to the recipient (SfPYZfxM8r, PRIVASIS Enterprise).
    - The repetition count at which memorized PII becomes extractable (P3kH8fecLl).
    - Secure transformer inference under FHE (xvp4bV32jk).
- **2027 papers are more methodologically self-critical: they report null controls, pre-registration and reproduction bugs.**
  - ContamMorph traces an MIA "gain" to a zero-denominator choice in released code (tdgQHe4jQ0).
  - Emotion steering is checked against random-direction controls (cnt2K3pBY7).
  - A pre-registered randomized experiment on algorithmic price collusion (loc4QSnO7D).
  - 72-run fine-tuning studies of abstention (KcAC7CBxd2).
- **Unlearning and provenance expand to new modalities.**
  - Unlearning:
    - Speaker-identity unlearning in audio LLMs (uPIlK5UOFC).
    - Unlearning in flow-matching models (RVTf5BFDDV).
    - Concept erasure in T2V models by grafting frames (U7ONNWWUMA).
    - Comparing the internal states that unlearning methods produce (1GDVZNoF1j).
  - Provenance and detection:
    - The VidTide living benchmark (April 2026 slice) shows a large gap between static benchmarks and in-the-wild video (VLOprj6bRK).
    - Detecting AI-generated images through knowledge inconsistencies (8QXGLg69Hh).
    - Ownership watermarks carried by which valid solution a model returns (RypqWww4nY).
- **Recurring models and benchmarks.**
  - 2027: Qwen3/Qwen3.5, Qwen3-VL, Gemma-4, GPT-5.6, Claude Sonnet 5 / S.4.5, Terminal Bench, StrongREJECT, AdvBench and PrivacyLens.
  - 2026: Qwen3-4B, LLaVA-1.5, GPT-4o, POPE/MMVet and NavSim.
