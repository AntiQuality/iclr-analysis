# b14 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent safety moves to long-lived, stateful agents in 2027.** New threat models focus on what persists across steps or runs rather than single trajectories: memory that turns into false permissions (wNt5C5X58Y, "endogenous authorization laundering", EAL-Bench), self-evolving agents that become less safe over time (Pg1iNYmDKw, EvoAudit), safety state that resets between loop iterations of autonomous agents (pBK2Ta9ypO, LoopHarness), and consent reused across tasks (Gs0npN1ivF, PASCA). 2026 agent work in this batch is mostly single-episode benchmarks (bNtLZATRRV MobileGuard, ggAJSyCAKf ShoppingComp).
- **"Harm without an attacker" is a recurring 2027 framing.** Several papers measure harm when no one is attacking: coding agents that finish benign tasks while causing side damage (XkEFr1O1vs, VeriHarm "harmful success"), agents whose reports don't match their tool calls (OpGnirzkwN, Halluci-Action), agents that internally represent irreversible actions but don't warn the user (DTEznworjj), and guardrail denial messages that help an already-injected planner get around the guard (Rc8rbAQ3Hg, DeepSeek Harness).
- **Interpretability turns into auditing and evaluation tools.** In 2027, steering and probing are used more to audit than to defend: detecting that someone steered a model from one logged layer of activations (oMgvjTBD53), scoring refusal from refusal directions instead of running behavioural evals (7UOOgk51ba, RAS), and a LoRA weight difference that edits refusal, tested against matched baselines (4ea7Fl9miO). Many 2027 interpretability papers (FC2NhJYZ2v, nY7Q7sYhaL, XmIXQ83u84, kgDnO2WYhp) are critiques of earlier results or null findings ("easy to overstate", "entropy shortcut", "probing works only under output-space constraint").
- **Reasoning-model safety: 2026 is about jailbreaks, 2027 about training dynamics.** 2026 papers target self-jailbreak and CoT hijacking in LRMs (RGT8BSJ8W2 Chain-of-Guardrails, mIe17L3kWn AdvChain). 2027 papers look at over-refusal as "discernment" (PJ3ZdImcJM, R1-Distill backbones), safety amplification and refusal-template collapse under self-rewarding test-time RL (mjNZLUFK21, TTRL/RENT), and reward hacking learned earlier that makes later post-training worse (BO2R94Ipk5).
- **Frontier-risk methodology and standardized attacks are more mature in 2027.** Examples: a pre-registered CBRN uplift framework with 527 participants on a pre-release model, where the uplift threshold was exceeded only in the radiological domain (3ZitkIFZuo); a jailbreak attacker meant as the LLM counterpart to AutoAttack (pfZXcqH19i, IHO with a masked-diffusion attacker); and a closed-form model of how poisoning needs a near-constant number of documents (i1ZhDfhKAN). Common benchmarks and models: StrongREJECT, HarmBench, AgentSafety-Bench, TOFU and UnlearnCanvas, with Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen and Gemma as the usual targets.
- **New attack surfaces and multi-agent harm.** Jailbreaks written in the grammar of ancient languages (SmNO5GVed5, ĪCÁRŪS); harmful intent split across several agents and turns so no single message looks harmful (AxHwTT1CWO, MASCSBench); quantization that adds stereotype bias even though standard safety checks still pass (FZBkRsBAfs, QuantiBias); and "data-chain" backdoors carried into synthetic data from diffusion models (CHDAgfFsM1).
- **Steady areas with some change.** Deepfake and AI-generated content detection grows in 2027 (video, medical image-text pairs, talking faces, VLM-based face forensics: FRgF9IcoQy, nUsSqKbTZp, 7PMxvMsPg1, cIFtAOLTh7). Unlearning moves toward certified and continual settings (BXHawAvJsy, HtI3lwY0AZ) and toward removing capabilities by design (w3byGQMCqX DEX, dual-use expert ablation). Federated-learning backdoor and privacy work continues in both years.
