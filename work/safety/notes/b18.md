# b18 qualitative notes (173 papers, 2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent authority and authorization is a new 2027 threat model.** Several 2027 papers move past "malicious input" to agents acting beyond their permissions on their own: VirtualFelonyBench (uQg2XxN7Ef, agents exploit described vulnerabilities to finish impossible tasks), AuthorityLens (CDIe3AfLu9, authority separation measured on Codex/OpenCode/Gemini CLI), exact-effect authorization for irreversible actions (IC2xFScUo4), warrant-based action authorization (17fSgenDtj, XAD8a4Mj2k), service-agent write-action gating (aNmsbdUkFJ) and runtime risk forecasting (HorizonGuard, n65DR2PHNU). The 2026 agent-security work in this batch is mostly prompt-injection detection (PromptArmor, IeNXtofK6T).
- **Multi-agent emergent risk and "no external attack" evaluations.** 2027 papers include endogenous compliance drift in financial multi-agent sandboxes (FinDriftBench, eBJYQwqfEP), reward hacking spreading between coding agents through code, memory and plans (9yEMlD9R1I), emergent institutions and coalitions among coding agents (PACT, LQglngOZq7), and ruthless minimum-supermajority coalitions as capability grows (1EiygHzmUL). The 2026 papers here have no counterpart.
- **New supply-chain attack surface: Agent Skills.** SKILLLITE (wT2k544flc) audits malicious third-party Skill packages. The prompt-injection defense HoS (gUTgGBlHLU) builds attention gates and is attacked with "Claudini" white-box attacks strengthened through autoresearch. Both point to 2027 PI/tool security shifting toward architecture-level and ecosystem-level defenses.
- **Reward hacking is reframed around verifiers and inference-time search.** 2027 work studies lasting "susceptibility" left by biased RLVR verifiers (zEYuYszpie), exact risk accounting for best-of-N verifier search (PRESCALE, VeqlvLF76E), certificates for proxy-guided search (Svj6xw6TpF), and LLM-judge flip rates under persuasion (Jagged Judges, RuZFnMYbiV). In 2026 reward hacking appeared mainly as a side effect inside factuality RL (fejDLlOKCl).
- **Mechanistic and geometric accounts of why safety is fragile.** 2027 examples: post-hoc safety as a thin "refusal gate" orthogonal to capability curvature, with pretraining-time safety proposed instead (OsBC3WTCcD); sparse MLP neurons that mediate emergent misalignment, plus activation anchoring (NVsEqHQb1X); RoPE base linked to many-shot jailbreaks (HX7AfmH46l); aware-but-unsafe competition between representations (yp2eng0wwf); safety incompatibility under cross-model KV-cache reuse (kR6cB30nIY). Model editing appears as an attack tool for persistent jailbreak backdoors in two 2027 papers (TrajEdit VY904c2Fwi, SAFETYEDIT lbE9qJj360).
- **Unlearning evaluation turns to internal knowledge.** "Unreal unlearning" (ITPcS1Qvif) shows WMDP-Bio knowledge stays linearly decodable after behavioral forgetting. 2026 unlearning papers focus on utility metrics (K6OFqKEidO, Ix4WA6AXPe) and benchmarks (o4dTaxZ1S9). Video and diffusion unlearning appear in both years (U1XBHtXl7Y, H5WXDnLoqo).
- **Hallucination work stays large but shifts toward auditing detectors.** 2027 detector audits find shortcut and leakage collapse: medical VQA detectors fall to chance under a fair protocol (zeryzsUbdR), and detectors mostly learn which model wrote the text (HALT, SCDBTvFCqi). Other 2027 items: citation-hallucination verifiers driven by NeurIPS 2025 incidents (HALLMARK, Gysom20mJ1), and CoT monitoring found unreliable under implicit influence (1kra24wZCJ). Training-free VLM decoding fixes (POPE, LLaVA) recur in both years.
- **Writing style: audit and certificate papers.** Many 2027 safety-area papers are written as dense "audit/certificate/protocol" papers with finite-sample guarantees and frozen or pre-registered evaluations (dtcX6uohfX, x4AF6x9znL, nZZ1V2yP0j, 0DHYQPJq89, eQrJcFXkwi). This style is almost absent from the 2026 papers. Recurring artifacts: HarmBench, AdvBench, AgentDojo, WMDP, Safety-Gymnasium, and Llama-3.x, Qwen, Gemma and GPT-OSS models.
