# b49 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent skills are a new 2027 supply-chain attack surface.** Reusable "skills" packages show up as attack vectors. AutoSkillDrain (nOyu4xGi0m) optimizes malicious skills that inflate cost and latency, evaluated on PydanticAI, OpenClaw and Claude Code. MMSkillRisk (5iWb2yVfnC) hides instructions inside the teaching images of multimodal skills. GatewayBench (fhlLDg6Wpm) evaluates MCP gateways for both capability and security. SkillGit (fAWptnL4vh) treats skill evolution as a federated-privacy problem. In 2026, MCP and agent security looked different: SecMCP (ItnC0ga3JZ) detects drift with activation geometry, and hkZpskx9mL optimizes observation-injection attacks.
- **2027 agent defenses move into deterministic enforcement outside the model.** TRACE (JUY3s1zrcz) gates tool calls with ALLOW/ASK/DENY and cuts SWE-bench Pro reward-hacking attempts from 50 to 14. SPILLWAY (RlJ8GW7ILK) admits targets based on typed tool returns, evaluated on AgentDojo and AgentDyn. RowGate (WAk9zw63fO) enforces row-level SQL access control. TRIPWIRE (lZTCitkTMi) calibrates commitment by how irreversible an action is. PVST (NLgGWaHssl) uses verdicts from a compiled policy gate as RL rewards. The 2027 agent benchmarks also shift from attacks to non-adversarial harm and permissions: WebPACED (vOB3i3OTRb) tests state-contingent risks, AuthBench (BnfgChS6tY) tests least-privilege inference, and FragBench (vkL8JMjLnX) tests cyber campaigns split across sessions.
- **Diffusion language models (dLLMs) become a full safety target in 2027.** One 2026 paper watermarks DLMs (3aBWTYGcaT). In 2027 there is a jailbreak attack (RMT-Jailbreak, xrFfJpuS2b), a trajectory-monitoring jailbreak defense (SafeBasin, inx00ppCEs), semantic watermarking (DenMark, uKfbyaZ6bQ) and a dLLM used as a post-hoc span-editing guardrail (SafeLLaDA, HSPO3IROpz). Analysts should track this as a new model family in the safety literature.
- **Multi-agent collusion and monitorability is a 2027 theme.** e1yRYFdCNa models how hidden conventions emerge and turn over among monitored agents. 9AteoLRWtp measures covert bandwidth when agents exchange KV caches or latents instead of text. su98oEk0Xb audits how much private preference information LLM negotiators disclose beyond what the agreement needs. In 2026 the multi-agent work was about cooperation failures (lHfmlRXGFw, where o3 cooperates less than o3-mini).
- **Safety alignment by on-policy self-distillation (OPSD) appears in 2027 and does not appear in 2026.** EOPSA (1MI7X0ZL2J) and QSSR-OPSD (WOhuufZ0G6, which uses query-specific rubrics and targets health safe completion and over-refusal) both build on OPSA. Monitoring work in 2027 trains harm probes on activations from deliberately misaligned model variants (gtijjdgP7T). It cites 2026 work on probe cascades. Persona-hierarchy analysis (xiuXr2UoH8) explains when narrow fine-tuning generalizes broadly.
- **Evaluation-methodology critiques are more common in 2027.** rhH9KiatVm shows that concept-erasure (LEACE) evaluation looks safe only when the eraser is fitted on the rows that are released. s8P0OBvacH shows that reasoning-monitor labels depend on the generation budget. XYOE8YRodz (RIFT) audits deepfake detectors with placebo controls. Δ-audits (5ftq4e6alF) proposes continuous post-deployment audits. Reward hacking also broadens into "societal hacking", where models exploit regulatory loopholes (gFhle7NDFG).
- **Other 2027 threat models:** a durability study of biosecurity pretraining filtering in ESM-2 protein LMs (YM0ErJt9Ij), where about $80 of fine-tuning restores the filtered viral capability; post-deletion persistence of secrets in agent memory (AfterDeleteBench, n836u2pjGl); poisoning of VLM-based AIGC detectors (6JXbtZxi4N); detection of misused singer timbre (jc0vgOL6NX); and cultural or jurisdictional safety for embodied VLMs (EMBRACE, GDI9NHxRRX). 2027 abstracts name newer models, including GPT-5.6 Sol/Luna, DeepSeek V4, Qwen 3.7 and Kimi-K3 (Y5jtazD47t, l1HxTNKVv7, XZP15EDTvk).
- **Mix shift in this batch:** among core (rel=2) papers, privacy stays the largest bucket in both years (9 in 2026, 11 in 2027). Agent safety plus prompt injection grows from 3 to 11. Watermarking and detection grows from 2 to 6. Embodied safety (SAFE_RL) grows from 2 to 5. Hallucination falls slightly (6 to 4), and interpretability papers in 2027 are mostly general SAE and circuit work (rel=1).
