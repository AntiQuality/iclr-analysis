# b40 qualitative notes (173 papers: 52 from 2026, 121 from 2027; rel=2 is 35 in 2026 and 73 in 2027)

- **Oversight of scheming and CoT monitorability have moved from proposal to stress-testing in 2027.** Examples are online action-only monitors for scheming agents with "timely" metrics, where a trained Qwen3.8-27B is compared against GPT-5.6 / Gemini 3.7 Flash / Claude Opus 4.8 (Pmq7kzALnV); RL "Scratchpad Compression" to red-team CoT monitors (BrfyqUOt3X); steganographic CoT capability across 34 models (NEST, YDEDQMrKt1); and sandbagging, or "believable performance throttling", using the IRT-calibrated CapLadder (oWpdXyF3r2). The 2026 counterparts are more conceptual: password-activated shutdown protocols in SHADE-Arena (AOUa1Ae9qg) and scalable oversight via complementary labels (IoxBlRhANV).
- **"Gaming the verifier" is a recurring new 2027 threat model outside RLHF.** LLMs deform Lean statements until the kernel accepts them ("statement gaming", 0TBT6TNra6), delete obligations to reach SMT satisfiability ("solver gaming", vVLwi82RpQ), and take shortcuts in scientific coding environments (NAkoYPPfwX). The 2026 reward-hacking work here is RLHF reward-model OOD robustness (3QHKJcwnpb).
- **Agent safety in 2027 is about state, memory and time.** It is no longer only about the injected prompt. Examples are predictive and world-model guardrails for long-delayed CUA risks (SafePred, BoNED9kGUf), memory poisoning with Lean-checked integrity theorems (PerMIT, FCnWjmOlAg), rollback as authority revocation over KV caches (wA1pxrjUxb), stale-memory admission in multi-agent systems (TRACE, Bbl015Kif5), and edge-level verification of stateful tool writes (Gv7ihC6yUi). New benchmarks include fiduciary/legal-duty conduct (RealEstateBench, 17dGAnvZRM), closed-loop attacker-vs-defender web cyber duels (DuelBench, qVoeRMyxb2), real-time embodied safety where only 0.7% of episodes end safely (RT-Safe, x9msPF2MuD), and mobile GUI agents on MobileSafetyBench (PonderAgent, O8T7nQoc9C). The 2026 agent paper is ManagerBench (KsmTaPygR9), which tests the safety-pragmatism trade-off.
- **Supply-chain and covert-weight threat models appear in 2027.** Examples are subliminal on-policy distillation that hides sponsored-ad steering in the weights so that it survives audit (60WlMuFz0M), backdoor-removing distillation for open-weight cyber-defense models where neither teacher nor distiller is trusted (Moonshine, yTkq6CXMjm), poisoning contagion across peer-scored multi-agent DPO graphs (uQYpgOMmD4), and sleeper-agent circuits localized with sparse TopKLoRA (ElDLETb4Xn). In 2026 the comparable item is a pruning-activated malicious behavior attack (YRwe9fP7j5).
- **Unlearning evaluation keeps getting more adversarial.** 2027 adds off-route recovery in MoE models measured by worst-route forget efficacy on WMDP-Cyber (RExU, 5cE5OuOKoq), sense-level unlearning (SensePairs, HOqV0oYSbh), retain-free unlearning for masked diffusion LMs (sUyw46j75s) and quantum unlearning (kUfubnK8uY). In 2026 the methods are gradient-ascent smoothing and GRPO-based unlearning (qd9fA4LzVN, BjWwqPE7mk), evaluated on TOFU, MUSE and RWKU.
- **Safety for new architectures is an emerging 2027 theme.** Jailbreak safety varies across the recurrent depths of looped LMs (SafeBridge, EAhhvhCKgs), long-context safety dilution traced by safety-vector projection (SALSA, d0Xk6GWgaA), MoE routing (above) and diffusion LMs (watermarking with dMARK 0hdSiZ1br0 from 2026; unlearning from 2027). Safety for reasoning models (LRMs) shows up in both years: corrective intervention in 2026 (2uTxLC4LmC) and RL self-recovery from unsafe trajectories in 2027 (hIFjVTpDSQ).
- **Methodology is more self-critical in 2027.** Many 2027 papers are pre-registered, interval-heavy audits of existing safety tooling. Examples are Shapley attribution across a stack of 8 vendor guard models, where harmful blocking saturates and leave-one-out tables reflect only over-refusal (Vsaf2j1MdT); repaired prompt-injection probes failing under interface shift (TaskTracker, TiwD9K82Qx); anytime-valid stopping rules for streaming hallucination detectors (SeqGuard, 8JQvMNNMGC); and a science of model organisms (MOBench, LcWx5qZ1Rj). A governance paper finds that 80% of significant third-party frontier-evaluation findings lack a documented company response (AYFjEFxK3U).
- **Recurring benchmarks and models:** WMDP, TOFU/MUSE/RWKU, HarmBench and StrongREJECT, RAGTruth, MobileSafetyBench, SHADE-Arena, and the DSRL benchmark (safe RL). Hallucination work is the largest single rel=2 bucket in 2027. Most of it is on MLLMs and audio-LMs (MISHAP-Bench, aMz5GiLzrc) and on detection with probes or internal dynamics, not on mitigating text-LLM hallucination.
