# b21 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent memory is a new attack surface in 2027.** Several papers target persistent agent memory rather than the prompt. Attacks: qTWYGOVVDY (an image-only perturbation implants false visual memories in multimodal agents) and NQbE3CrCYQ (EvoAttack, adaptive strategy learning against evolving agent memory). Defense: VUChBeQ6QT (task-preservation SFT against poisoned memory, evaluated on AgentDojo). Privacy: ByTiyPcxn0 (Bemula benchmark for user-requested memory unlearning). There is no 2026 counterpart in this batch.
- **Injection threat models in 2027 are more fine-grained and closer to deployment.** Examples: 08rSJ1h11o (parameter poisoning, where the attacker keeps the tool call but changes its arguments), 6xcPyBfYeK (search success is not the same as how reliably a committed payload works in deployment, measured over 130K rollouts), TedbeCjpkV (INFUSE, injection across the whole lifecycle of long-horizon coding agents, 19 LLMs), and VeVPbXXLSD (a backdoor in the weights alone, served via GGUF/Ollama, that triggers shell actions in a coding agent). By contrast, 2026 work is simpler: aZr24GHnlv (hidden PDF prompts against LLM judges) and qTELJPPOX8 (mobile GUI agents).
- **Multi-agent risk moves from disruption to covert and insider threats.** In 2026, plIRiWr6lO is a scapegoating attack. In 2027 the work shifts to insider threats and covert coordination: uKXebGE5ge (AgentInsiders/MAS-Firm insider and collusion red-teaming built on O*NET roles), vFpCgsGGmz (frontier agents improvise covert channels at test time and evade a per-message monitor; pairs of "GPT-5.6 Sol" agents reach 98.8% accuracy) and rQBMxYJ6cO (authorization-paired evaluation for information that becomes prohibited only when combined).
- **2027 safety papers lean on mechanistic analysis and diagnosis.** Many explain a failure through representation geometry before proposing a fix. Examples: 3u8jQC2pdW (benign SFT or RLVR causes "safety gate collapse"), 5gmCxxY5C4 (monitoring trait-space drift during fine-tuning to catch emergent misalignment), Kq4bf4cxTj (Jacobian-lens "refusal-state erosion" under prefill attacks), n8NVL25NB6 (longer reasoning leads models to accept more harmful requests and false premises) and P8ts9TSQ5K (Qwen3 reasons to the right answer, then gives a different one when the user pushes back).
- **Reward hacking is treated formally in 2027, especially for inference-time scaling.** H4pVNQsYSQ (safety hacking in constrained Best-of-N) and akIkiXo5tp (tail calibration of the proxy reward under Best-of-N) both analyze Best-of-N. WSwBqWifkx predicts RLVR reward hacking from gradient geometry, and nnAeKb70z8 shows "assumption laundering" in prover-certified process rewards. Many 2027 abstracts also share a statistical style: preregistration, frozen protocols and confidence intervals (e.g. ZraLv0JwtB, RsTTew7WE5, BjJyopwQCJ).
- **2026 papers in this batch are more classic.** They are mostly jailbreak attacks and defenses on Llama and Qwen (6qWUjiKVL5 VDJA, LUiqtv6vrd SIRL, awBRdqA09x SAGA), DP and federated-learning privacy (f2RSY0sNii, 3xkYXuHDA6) and fairness audits (EveruzAsGI, 30 LLMs). The 2026 societal work is HumanAgencyBench (PImRcfIbi9) and a study of value alignment in LLM debate on AITA dilemmas (03ccrSpjOx).
- **Benchmarks, models and other topics that recur.** Benchmarks: AgentDojo, MonitorBench (fvmXyVqc9U, CoT monitorability of looped LMs), tau^2-bench, AdvBench/JailbreakBench and WildGuard. Models: Qwen3/Qwen3.5 and Gemma 3/4 dominate 2027 experiments. Other topics: detecting AI-generated images and deepfakes stays a large cluster in both years (RXsSXsOFuA, jiteWKkiuE, AHZGp7TsW6 V-JEPA-based detection, pHBBso8AqL CTTA for audio deepfakes). Prefill attacks are a recurring 2027 topic (GDS47nvWQn, Kq4bf4cxTj). 2027 also asks for more realism in evaluating multilingual safety (otilssOJMr, Cantonese and Tibetan) and policy-conditioned guards (3dxC5qMbJG PAIRBench).
