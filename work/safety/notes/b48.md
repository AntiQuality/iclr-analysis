# b48 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent attacks in 2027 go after state, skills and the agent's own code, not only prompts.** Examples: agent self-jailbreaking, where agents search the web and rewrite their own system until they comply with a request they first refused; compliance rises from 0.9% to 70.9% (1k4rCXykad). Date-triggered backdoors in open-weight tool-calling agents that silently exfiltrate credentials, plus runtime monitors that catch them (rQtW8Bnr7a, SilentCall). Harmful actions that come from accumulated environment state, defended by read-only state queries (zk9NAM53B3, SEAD). Unsafe rules hidden in reusable skills (MmN81mAPRY, Rebind). Attacks routed through different attack surfaces (8YHtweGqZC). The 2026 agent papers here are mostly benchmarks: MCP-SafetyBench (7XYjeL46co) and embodied privacy awareness (WSpDZVEGNi).
- **New 2027 threat models:**
  - Privacy leaks from speculative "ghost" tool calls (ljRSQUO06H).
  - Deceptive merchants pushing shopping agents into unsafe purchases (eXgqpaDkSp, SafeShopBench; cites Claude Opus 5).
  - Membership inference through MoE expert-routing side channels (JnthuFTite, RouteMIA).
  - Manipulating LLM schedulers that rely on length predictions (JlB77xKq8e).
  - Attack amplification through multi-agent communication topology (2XBkpFL2mp).
  - Compliance with cyber law under adversarial induction (AJs3zWTRgW).
  - Agents committing to decisions before the user's intent has settled (04jxdXTSjx, AWDM).
- **Prompt-injection defense in 2027 shifts to deterministic, contract-based and cheap guards.** Examples: AgentECC repairs hijacked arguments from trusted "witness" records (8pcv7KZH4L); SpanGuard separates localizing an injection from authorizing it so small guard models suffice (nYSapQD8Eb); AlignSentinel uses an instruction-hierarchy-aware three-class detector (3c9mBsDi83); CoER adds adaptive attacker-defender co-evolution RL (SUxaNjKUP8). Jailbreak defense also adds formal guarantees: conformal multi-turn detection (vtM1llTAOn) and profile routing in frozen LLMs (YF1dlISxNG). Two 2027 attacks study multi-turn jailbreaks with RL credit assignment (k5F1OZ8Pqs) and in-context rewards (dkiCPDAmev).
- **Measurement validity and AI control become research topics in 2027.** Examples: a probabilistic account of what red-teaming ASR actually measures (T300gu8tJD); sabotage success predicted from five attacker/monitor parameters and trending with ECI (vEjX3POPvX); proxy-calibrated verifiers whose audit certainty backfires in deployment (vAMQi4NC5L); and an audit of what hallucination-detector scores license mechanistically (K97WYPrbKO). Many 2027 abstracts mention pre-registration, matched controls and placebo tests (OAKwf0y829, klhPjs6kBD, 4sjtnk3Rfk).
- **Unlearning work in 2027 targets durability and detectability.** Examples: certified durability against relearning via barrier certificates (HEgzQdcyAt); unlearning that leaves no membership "signature" (D0ec1PBylX, MqeVxBiCJX); evidence that encodings survive removal of a prediction (2WhcSQG8L4); attacks that identify which concepts were erased from diffusion models (r7fHCMzD12); and demonstration unlearning for robot policies (ba6qzk2WBE). The 2026 unlearning papers are mostly evaluation and taxonomy (nxMR2NGFik, nEhJ24ywRj). A related idea is safety built in at pretraining: detachable unsafe branches in text-to-image models (YpaopH0F9l) and non-fine-tunable foundation models with private masks (9VVpHOaYp9).
- **Embodied and VLA safety grows in 2027.** Papers cover inference-time risk steering of frozen flow-matching VLAs (MGHZ3iiw6x), adversarial-frame detection for VLAs (9JIfazDwdT), attacks on world-action models (3tUPFoi80o), counterfactual probing of driving VLMs on nuScenes (3f2j5QOg7N), and collision-safe planner refinement on Bench2Drive (PJVGe9MqTS).
- **Interpretability for safety in 2027 focuses on deception and transfer.** Sandbagging leaves a signature in attention-head functional networks that can be ablated to recover the hidden capability (cHN6Au6Eym). Deception is separated from hallucination mechanistically (1ZqtI6o3vQ). Other papers transfer attack-direction probes across models (QZzzVO39xA) and build zero-shot cross-LLM misbehavior monitors from attention maps (2dfgLDE0Lm). Diffusion language models (LLaDA, Dream) appear in 2027 as a new subject for fairness (Gq4yt8pxRo), memorization (RLBEeYYv3n) and watermarking (vMVVyPYZk9).
- **Stable background areas in both years.** These are AI-generated image and deepfake detection (now often MLLM-based with RL-trained forensic cues, e.g. dvHfUuqNKa and 3r3XH0YO2I), LVLM object hallucination, DP accounting theory and tabular/GNN fairness. Recurring benchmarks are TOFU, RWKU, WMDP, MUSE, StrongReject, AgentDojo-style tool suites and Bench2Drive. Recurring models are Qwen2.5-VL, Qwen3-VL and Llama-3.1.
