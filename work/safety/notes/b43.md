# b43 notes (173 papers)

- **Agent risk moves past prompt injection in 2027.** New threat models include resource-exhaustion attacks that bloat the KV cache (tiQI0BTKmo, KOVER), safety loss in self-evolving agents caused by accumulated memory (20aqR8E6En, EvoSteer, which corrects it with activation steering), and authorization that changes as user instructions are revised (uyKTT6RLRr). Other examples are malicious "agent skills" detected with learned rules (PHzJd1tMEr, MalSkillBench), auditing of agent harnesses for permission and information-flow violations partway through a trajectory (TmzpU43YEd), agents that over-rely on corrupted tool outputs (NpQjihOVvb), and Shapley-guided collusive red-teaming of hierarchical multi-agent systems (wkTsyrayxJ). The 2026 agent papers in this batch are mostly direct attacks: code-agent jailbreaks (dpVhlf13tk), adversarial images on web agents (AMIb7RWnBI) and manipulation of tool selection (dQXa5jvpQN).
- **Evaluation now tracks whole trajectories and checks behaviour against judgment.** BLINDSPOT (NZZVoZd92H) scores long-horizon tool use with a five-way outcome that includes over-refusal. SameFact (tKp1H9Jz0L) finds that safety judgments do not carry over to the actions a model actually chooses. DRaligned (wtcXi9EEz7) tests how deep-research agents behave after the user steps away. All three favour execution-grounded counterfactual protocols over measuring attack success rate.
- **Control, oversight and honesty are a growing 2027 cluster.** CIAware-Bench (H2Vu07MLhq) asks whether models notice when an AI-control protocol has intervened, using BigCodeBench, Bash Arena and SHADE-Arena. Related papers bound CoT monitorability and post-train for it (jIW6GzEeDj), measure self-verification on problems the model cannot solve as a scalable-oversight test (ATOgX1aPfO), and check whether linear probes transfer across post-training stages (LHVG1HENCR). Another counts "silent restoration": a wrong CoT step followed by a correct answer (tK4yRayph8). The 2026 precursors here are SRFT self-report of hidden objectives (sWs0cCuM8I) and inoculation prompting (RgOHRYHX5L).
- **Interpretability for safety in 2027 is more cautious about its own methods.** Several papers test whether earlier detectors really work. One applies negative controls to reward-hacking detectors that read activations, and finds that base models reproduce most of the signal (CluaSO1lR9). Another runs a stability study of truth and safety probes under the PCS framework (iW2C4wQujV). A third shows that the batch-norm statistics shipped with checkpoints change verdicts on MU-Bench unlearning (GDws8Tm7j9). Sleeper agents and emergent misalignment are now standard model organisms: feature-resolved attention is used to steer sleeper agents (YCJoDWuaOv), and EM is shown to shift internal truth representations (Ch5ZaFjOHu).
- **Several frontier models from 2027 are named.** One paper evaluates Claude 5 Sonnet, Muse Spark 1.0, Gemini 3.5 Flash, GPT-5.6 Terra/Sol and Grok 4.6 on mining conversation histories to find harmful compliance and over-refusal (vizO6rD25H). CIAware-Bench reports that awareness of control interventions rises sharply for GPT-6 Astra and Claude 5 (Fable 5, Opus 5). Open-weight work mostly uses the Qwen3/Qwen3.5/Qwen3-VL, Gemma-3, OLMo-3 and gpt-oss families.
- **New attack surfaces in the model supply chain.** These include backdoors triggered by quantization (hgUngr5Oxt, ReQOR), backdoors that spread through decentralized LoRA exchange (R4w7JyOVrz), detection of backdoored LoRA adapters from their weights alone (4FoPPXCNB1), and jailbreak backdoors trained with GRPO and paired feedback (O0sVuhVTxB). Provenance work extends to detecting distillation (wfipaAhV3Z), antidistillation of reasoning traces (SFMJPriDVw, 2026) and fingerprinting under stochastic decoding (MfgDKKxrns).
- **Watermarking and multimodal hallucination remain large, steady clusters.** Watermarking now reaches new generators: continuous diffusion LMs (6SzEZJld4O), pairwise distortion-free text watermarks (0zVboRFeK6) and multi-bit multiplexed text watermarks (m7U66QaItd). Work on MLLM hallucination is moving from contrastive decoding toward mechanistic diagnosis (Z9LIgXgr7j, the Grounded Control Reversal failure mode) and theory (Jehk8zMDeh shows calibrated VLMs must hallucinate). Biosecurity alignment of protein language models (ewC9iwU1BC, ESM3/PoET-2) is a new item in the dangerous-capability area.
