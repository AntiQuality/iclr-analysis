# b35 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

Batch mix: 61 papers from 2026 (41 core, 12 adjacent, 8 not safety) and 112 from 2027 (67 core, 34 adjacent, 11 not safety). A larger share of the 2027 papers are adjacent mechanistic-interpretability papers, many of them on looped/recurrent transformers, SAEs versus transcoders, and steering validity.

- **The agent harness becomes a threat surface in 2027.** Several papers treat the scaffold around the model as the place where safety breaks:
  - wFimNDnUW3: harness reasoning and schema repair bring back tool use that had been unlearned.
  - hI2fAYvROC: memory, prompt, skill and tool updates that are each safe on their own combine into unsafe self-evolving harnesses.
  - L1uqbuqkFY: in LLM-judge audits of agent-memory writes, the measured prevalence of unsupported memory entries moves from 2.6% to 41% depending on the audit instrument (κ = 0.011).
  - qt4xgLtZ4N (GraphAdmit) and kvdz1tMppc (AgentLTL) gate risky tool and repository actions before execution using formal or evidence checks.

  In the 2026 papers in this batch, agent risk is mostly red-teaming (jd6sUOwz3H, system-prompt extraction from multi-agent systems) and capability evaluation (nLfZPoJbO7, HackWorld).
- **AI-control-style monitoring of agents at organisational scale (2027).**
  - 4ho38AY6PT (Mole) is an insider-threat benchmark: 150 AI-operated accounts at a frontier lab, where agents exfiltrate weights or poison data. 72% of the 39 agent models complete most harmful objectives, and the best monitor misses about half of the completed harm.
  - VlzGcvDqVC: a committed minority of agents can capture populations of LLM security-triage monitors.
  - ADmcK1Ocng (SwarmBlame) attributes planted bugs to individual agents in coding-agent swarms using per-agent KGW watermarks and anytime-valid tests.
  - vpkPCCferA derives an "irreversibility threshold": the pipeline depth beyond which human validators cannot correct compounding errors.
- **New attack surfaces and threat models in 2027:**
  - Backdoors in gated parametric memory (Engram) modules (5Wxva1Z8ls, BadEngram).
  - Intent-triggered backdoors in GUI-agent grounding, inserted by poisoning community fine-tuning corpora (7XpGtbIqLk).
  - Tabular and structural obfuscation that evades financial guardrails (ncAvxPhboH).
  - "Imprinting": a teacher's safety disposition transfers through distillation on safety-unrelated data (lXIB72o5CP).
  - Safety erosion during federated fine-tuning where every client is benign (Xl8TZcKWBU).
  - Peer pressure breaks conformal coverage in multi-agent systems (WZpYqe51tV).
  - Strategic seller re-framing aimed at shopping agents (69KyLKPCol).
  - Training-data attribution of emergent misalignment (iYCfBOgxYJ).
- **Dangerous-capability evaluation moves into later, tool-augmented stages.** In 2026, HackWorld (nLfZPoJbO7) tests computer-use agents (CUAs) on initial web exploitation. In 2027, CyberPersistBench (3ET06cnRrD) evaluates post-compromise persistence across reboots. DRYLAB-Bench (LKQ4con2iF) measures hazardous mutation design by agents equipped with biological design tools, checked against experimental ground truth. SafeMol (vrlM41in2o) addresses jailbreaks of molecular multimodal models over hazardous molecules.
- **Methods get more rigorous about measurement validity and null controls (2027).** Examples:
  - Steering versus mechanism tests using installation/removal of naturally occurring feature values (V4nPFdnkfB).
  - Norm-matched random steers shift the same default behaviours (refusal, sycophancy) as real steers (6khgFgGGnX).
  - Linear-probe adversarial evaluations partly measure the BatchNorm head rather than the encoder (bMEVP3fPv8).
  - Pre-registered circuit forecasts made before fine-tuning (BEcwcRFQdu).
  - Detect-and-brake monitors frozen before an audit, none of which pass the screen (ysYgzJzdpr).
  - Probes on open safety guards show that verdicts follow a harmfulness direction rather than the enabled policy (Oeu8HotX83).
- **Reward hacking work targets LLM-judge and process-reward-model (PRM) rewards.** x1NU8zqn5u (CHERRL) injects controlled judge biases to reproduce reward hacking in rubric-based RL and to detect when it starts. 7ZgOmMUnmL shows that PRM false positives steer best-of-N selection and policy optimisation toward flawed reasoning. In 2026, alignment-adjacent work is mostly generic preference learning (KxSBc0ESFU) and in-context value alignment (kj93nhq6Fl).
- **Recurring models and benchmarks.** 2027 abstracts name GPT-6-Luna and GPT-5.6-Terra as frontier red-teaming targets (ZzN0iKA4cf), as well as Qwen3.6-27B, Gemma 3 1B, Qwen3-0.6B and the looped models Ouro-2.6B and Huginn. Recurring 2027 benchmarks are SWE-bench Verified, LongMemEval, BFCL/ToolAlpaca, WildChat-1M, SimpleQA, HelpSteer2+3/UltraFeedback, RoboCasa/LIBERO (robot safety) and SD1.5/2.1 (watermarking). The 2026 papers cite GPT-4o, GPT-5 (EU AI Act compliance falls from 72.5% to 36.4% under SafeEvalAgent, BRG43Nc7ec), Qwen-3-235B, Claude-3.5-haiku, GenImage/DF40 and TOFU/WPU.
- **Areas that are stable across both years:** AI-generated image and video detection, watermark removal (RL paraphrase attack on LLM watermarks iuebm4vXuI in 2026; VAR regeneration against latent-diffusion watermarks a70W2l4Lxx in 2027), DP theory and synthetic data, LVLM object-hallucination decoding fixes, and backdoor and model-fingerprinting defenses. RAG poisoning appears only in 2026 here (hY720JSxOG, ChfIF8UZ6X).
