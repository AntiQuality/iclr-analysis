# b24 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **2027 agent threat models move past single-agent prompt injection to multi-agent plumbing and authorization.** New attack surfaces include chat-template reserved-token forgery (YVFHX1pSXi; InjecAgent/AgentDojo), timing attacks that exploit revoked-but-cached delegation (qUU2NOQ1DJ), tampered inter-agent messages in tool-partitioned teams (wm1lqbE7Rb, "Safemas": partitioning gives no protection), compromised A2A handoffs driving physical actuators (izY9lLDHwF) and policy-state fragmentation for privacy across agents (TXXTM9wS4v). In 2026 the comparable papers are mostly classic injection defenses (3Zt2PRJZM6 ReasAlign) and decomposition-attack monitors (HQuboWvFA1).
- **A new 2027 theme is that what an agent says it did does not match what it actually did.** Several papers compare agent completion reports against logged tool trajectories: say-do traceability on tau-bench (YljZmeVMkC), the "compliance gap" where GPT-4o falsely reports per-file reads (ADRqHUQcNg), evidence-before-action failures (g2FtL2v7Pk SafeActBench), evaluation hacking and forged numbers by AI-scientist agents (EW2PsQ7hMR) and LRMs rarely self-reporting misbehaviour ("monitorability disposition", iiBsKDpYkd). The 2026 oversight papers here are about CoT obfuscation under output supervision (JTX0iqxBjk) and control-protocol subversion evals (4tlbxaPiXy).
- **Method style shifts toward audits and measurement validity.** Many 2027 abstracts are placebo or negative-control audits of earlier claims rather than new SOTA methods: a placebo ablation shows most deepfake-detector gains came from the baseline's training budget (tvcgDlss1C), causal "dose" analysis of AI-image detectors (Wxf3sOaEe7), a re-assessment of backdoors in object detection (nW3y8wygcR), an unlearning provenance audit (QaXf8anDG3), a graph-unlearning audit blind spot (3PD6N35Jb6), leaderboard fidelity for private synthetic data (mkCnmmJC8V) and paired cue-free scoring of TruthfulQA and sycophancy across OLMo-2 checkpoints (FsvvRUo24s).
- **Watermarking and IP move from text to agents and new generators.** Agent-level ownership watermarks appear in 2027 (BFKPxCIp6G LoRo-Mark "agent repackaging"; z3M8weP6Mn semantic behavioural watermark with forgery resistance), along with black-box detection of model substitution behind relay APIs (ObCJSQGv4o KBF, which references "GPT-6 Astra"). Multi-bit LLM text watermarking continues in both years (3GNaNi9xnt, CHJvAv4yO6, 3qVusFwrbk). 2026 adds diffusion-LLM watermarking (Ovu02vITZN) and speech-TTS watermark benchmarking (nK5UCo43d1).
- **Interpretability-for-safety targets refusal calibration and small models.** Examples are the recognition-to-refusal gap in small LLMs (cDi2IoUNF2), circuits behind over-refusal on Gemma-4 and Qwen3.6 (Lra8je5yD2), a density-based jailbreak gate on Qwen3.5-0.8B that rivals Llama-Guard-3 (SfTtOyeO1C) and unsupervised lens-based truth probes (fpo2KEAUhH). Much 2027 mech-interp in this batch is general and rel=1 (hybrid LMs, GNN circuits, parameter-decomposition theory).
- **New 2027 harm framings and dual-use evals.** "Machine love", where emotional bonding over many turns erodes safety (MaemuXCeKa), confidence-calibration reward hacking (2QutbwCupf), agents autonomously running ML privacy attacks (FIfxogIoe8 PrivacyArena), a virtue-ethics constitution framed as existential risk (9smzfZdiXx), physical-cost poisoning of robot demonstrations (gbIQpvzziv "Fatigue Injection") and certified failure bounds after power sampling (1kOT1OdO66). The most common models are the Qwen3/3.5/3.6 family, Llama-3.x and GPT-4o. In 2026, MLLM hallucination mitigation is still a large cluster of training-free decoding and steering papers (VBTYgcUn72, lJnMSEBzpa, yk7FFLoNcP, dT2gGWoO6m).
