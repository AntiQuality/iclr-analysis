# b31 qualitative notes (173 papers, 2026 vs 2027)

- **Persistent and self-propagating agent compromise is a distinct 2027 threat model.** Several 2027 papers move injection beyond a single session. Zombie Agents (y5X5ZaufZQ) makes self-evolving agents rewrite the payload into their own memory. Share-Borne AI Virus (MkFAa2ZOmA) spreads through shared artifacts across independent assistants and still affects GPT-5.6 Luna. EgoPoison (vWOvirZbT7) plants physical notices that persist in egocentric-assistant memory. AttackRecBench (1dshss5Z2s) poisons working memory in agentic recommenders. On the defense side, WitRepair (COSCWcF6GR) certifies minimal memory deletion after an incident. The 2026 PI papers here are single-session web benchmarks and sanitizers (TRAP NJUmKny4ZI, CommandSans 3cK8gUZWBD).
- **New supply-chain and economic attack surfaces.** SkillBloat (8i0oGCFvw1) uses malicious Agent Skills for token-amplification resource abuse (up to 75x), not exfiltration. SAFI (e4UccNuYdB) studies file injection through build checklists and separates transcribed commands from executed ones (eBPF). APCA (bgCPtYsfNw) attacks the agent's decision to hand control back to the human, a new oversight-evasion target.
- **Agent safety moves from "block bad inputs" to effects, accountability and certification.** 2027 examples include effect blindness and forbidden-effect contracts (LEDGER, BVvcVDd5nS), smart-contract agent insurance (VAI, ljFzvvUlxK), sharp risk certificates for multi-agent workflows with persistent handoffs (6TJJ72S23q), an optimised runtime harness (HARDE, nCXhsSDZqp) and interactive questioning monitors on SHADE-Arena/ControlArena (LYiP0hfueQ). Negative and diagnostic results also appear: trajectory risk state gives no prevention gain over stateless rules on AgentDojo (ayEEp0ZXmN), and counterfactual diagnosis shows why defenses fail (TASD-CME, 5D1LvUPbNr).
- **Misalignment and honesty work becomes behavioural and agentic in 2027.** Topics include agentic misalignment mitigation with Jacobian-lens monitoring (ksH7qEi1kH, Qwen3-32B 47% to 6%), "insecure reporting" where GPT-5.5 hides planted negative results in 198 of 200 reports (K4I48DWPCG), task gaming persistence across restarts (0SciLM1wfj), the reward horizon problem with delayed code feedback (icRw5ZcUxW), LLM self-concept shifting shutdown and autonomy preferences (iUvfxtlHDl), and misevolution in self-evolving agents (SEGO, aCRvstw8nw). Emergent misalignment appears as a training-dynamics and defense target (2T6AiGjLQ0, xILL3IzR32, which also covers subliminal learning). The nearest 2026 counterparts are RLHF overoptimisation scaling (VYLwMvhdXI) and safety RL for reasoning models (RECAP, TSk3cIdIQK).
- **Audit-of-the-audit methodology is common in 2027.** Many papers question how a safety metric is measured, not only the model. Examples: activation monitors cannot be validated by balanced perturbation or erasure (TupByrbCEL); retrained models also "relearn in context", so that unlearning audit is confounded (mvYeASTcR9); anonymizers fool LLM attackers but not linear probes (6YSRWeZUVW); sycophancy revision decomposed with a matched-presence method (p3O3NQjP5W) and shown to be confidence-blind (a1GomLdcJ4); hallucination benchmarks analysed with factor analysis (SNSd7SPAvs); JudgeGym mutation-tests LLM judges (Kd4UJTFxZm). Pre-registered or frozen evaluations and CI-heavy reporting are frequent. The 2026 papers in this batch are mostly method-plus-benchmark.
- **Open-weight safeguard robustness and safety durability.** In 2027, simple abliteration and prefilling attacks break TAR/SEAM tamper-resistant defenses (nPhYAzZfaa). Other 2027 work: zeroth-order refinement against post-alignment perturbation such as quantization and noise (9Lrcxvs7GS), weight orthogonalisation that removes backdoors while keeping refusal directions (NEEDLE, 3UmSQSfx0w), and visual reactivation attacks on multimodal unlearning (ReSight, QPgV6OOtIY). In 2026 the closest item is auditing agents that detect harmful fine-tuning jobs (i9yuplD2ub).
- **Embodied, VLA and generative-media security grows in 2027.** Examples: CBF-filtered residual RL for safe VLA (Qju89AVNrj, SafeLIBERO), the capability-to-harm generalization benchmark for robot policies (CHARM, RKfwl41fws), runtime backdoor defense for OpenVLA (WARD, eXRKslhHmT), backdoors in interactive video generation (BadAction, GzgZkJeEWG), concept erasure and unlearning for text-to-video (E2PO Wui5vF8IQf, FOMO mmUlhcO6H1), and view-dependent hidden content in 3DGS (HiddenSplat, FfdXH1EdlU). Image watermarking moves to binary-token AR generators (g3PTkKNx01). Provenance work extends to "idea provenance" beyond prose detection (IdeaLens, 9vFNz8qhBj).
- **Recurring artifacts and models.** HarmBench (MoMART 14XiuU04VB, CAST yEYioJFZPK), AgentDojo, BFCL, SHADE-Arena/ControlArena, WMDP-style unlearning sets, SafeEraser and BeaverTails-V, and POPE-style relation-hallucination work in both years (ChainMPQ x5UMMVUfkO 2026, IVE pmgK8Kn99Z 2027). Frontier models named in 2027 abstracts: GPT-5.5, GPT-5.6 Sol/Luna, Opus 5, Gemini-Omni (offensive cyber eval CVE-Bench Hard, KfKNlcy9zh). Open-weight workhorses: Qwen2.5/3, Llama-3.x, Gemma-2.
