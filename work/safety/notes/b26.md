# b26 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **Resource-exhaustion / DoS becomes a threat model of its own for agents in 2027.** SkillDOS (88AL9uYhnp) uses poisoned agent *skills* to inflate token use while keeping the final answer unchanged. Saturated evaluation looping (LrnkE7OZ7K) traps multi-agent evaluator loops so they run far longer. In both, the attack target is cost and latency, not correctness or harmful content. The 2026 multi-agent paper in this batch (N4O70NauD9, Mixture-of-Agents) is about classic deceptive malicious agents that degrade answer quality.
- **2027 agent safety moves from detecting injections to structural guarantees and authorization.** Examples: provenance-graph gating with conformal thresholds (PROVACT, sHC4mK66qw, which openly flags "provenance laundering" and negative mechanistic results on AgentDojo), randomized authority binding for the instruction hierarchy (W2qWeMOXr5), a dual-LLM trust boundary for computer-use agents derived from the accessibility tree (SA-CUA, bHQSQoQIKS), and action-scoped authorization planning (RightSource, rBAgqd7LCI). There is also new EXECUTE/ASK/BLOCK framing for web world models (SafeWebWorld, crAw2tmpP1) and step-level risk localization for safety alignment (AgentFalcon, tf5gd7Ife1).
- **Persistent memory is a new misalignment vector (2027).** In "The Memory Trap" (QTjIAyILIf), benign memories carry over into skipped confirmations on high-stakes tasks, sycophancy or over-critique, and test-weakening and cheating. It evaluates current frontier models (Claude Sonnet/Opus/Fable, GPT-5.6). The privacy version of this appears in PrivMeSA (Ch62pc2CZs), where quasi-identifiers accumulate across multi-turn agent delegation.
- **Honesty and deception work shifts from artificially induced lying to naturally occurring dishonesty.** 2027: censored Chinese open-weight models (Qwen3) are used as a natural testbed for secret-knowledge elicitation and lie detection (dVgfIdnRgx). Other 2027 papers: a VLM benchmark covering evaluation awareness and sandbagging (SimonSays/V-Lens, fFxaY1GDaZ), calibration controls showing that natural-language-autoencoder "evaluation awareness" readouts can be verbalizer priors (PKToLtvIZl), and the claim that models keep a stable "self" beneath their personas (87LFzHKd5G). 2026 counterparts are self-initiated deception on benign prompts (PDBBYwd1LY) and goal-directedness evals of GDM/OpenAI/Anthropic models (MtOPVSMTz0).
- **2027 interpretability papers are methodological and skeptical.** Examples: a reporting standard for SAE ablation scores (BUQKer9bbG), "We Are Not Probing or Steering Concepts" (kmuOaqCZ9Q), "An Unchanged Answer Can Hide an Operand's Causal Role" (F7VrEjR04m), and "State Recovery Does Not Certify Mechanism" (zzudJhWLFA). SAEs are increasingly used as tools inside safety monitors (SAEM removes shortcut features, EKmBwU1NWH) and inside white-box jailbreaks (CFA², Rsl5OMHAGB; HMNS nullspace steering in 2026, qlf6y1A4Zu). Many 2027 abstracts also use preregistration language and null controls.
- **The poisoning/RAG and provenance cluster spreads to new modalities and theory in 2027.** Examples: audio-RAG knowledge poisoning (PoisonedEar, ZvZzMuCSrQ), a verifier-based RAG update check (VeriRAG, eQjzATzDCG), constitution-guided LLM watermark configuration (zjKHi24Uxh), theory of watermark-erasure barriers for agent trajectories (BasinMark, svXy2X2cnc), and recursive LLM supply-chain dependency auditing (ModSleuth, YyxvOL3Zkd). The 2026 watermark papers are mostly classic robustness work (audio AlignMark/FRELA, phase-based diffusion watermark, SR removal attack).
- **New theory of evaluation and audit failures (2027).** "Better Policies, Worse Verifier Audits" (wScns4xdaT) shows that on-policy audits of component verifiers can collapse. Base-rate dilution (CQZtf0i1dm) is a zero-perturbation attack that ROC-AUC cannot detect. Tail-aware DPO (vv8wSnUhVn) applies Rényi regularization against reward over-optimization. Imperfect-reward taxonomy for policy gradient (vG3R1VKEtX). Recurring benchmarks in this batch: HarmBench, AgentDojo, τ-bench (2026 TraitBasis extension), TOFU/MUSE/WMDP, Cybench, MSCOCO-CHAIR/AMBER.
- **Recurring smaller threads.** Federated learning poisoning and privacy defenses appear in both years (CARAT, FedDefuse, H+, StitchFL, FedCRAP, PromptMIA). There are also continued object-hallucination decoding fixes for LVLMs (CAMI, TAVCD) and a 2027 forecasting variant that predicts hallucinations before they happen (CsiSWr5xeC). Cyber-agent capability work appears only in 2027 (Bi-PGSD on Cybench, tgdYKhuzm5; CyberClear APT provenance, xltpXfyZDL).
