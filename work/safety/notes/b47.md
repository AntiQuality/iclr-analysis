# b47 notes (173 papers; 2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent attack surfaces move from prompts to persistent state and protocols (2027).** New threat models target state that changes over time: backdoors that only switch on once agent memory has evolved (4FHDyMlRYO, EvolvAttack), poisoned world models that steer planners (SW0VDPiwhS, CONMAN), LLMs whose own watermarked code output acts as an accumulating trigger (Tygnjcgtd2), and cross-user leakage through shared, evolving agent "skills" (0bPrFLWTjU, MASK-GATE). In 2026 the agent papers here are mostly IPI via tools/MCP (PFbt5pWWh7) and orchestrator leakage (eQ0bqlU7Zi).
- **MCP/tool-channel availability and authority attacks.** tUYDnsZ34t (2027) uses fake security alerts in MCP returns, turning safety refusals into a denial-of-service. quVsAPDOqR (2027) defines IPI by *authority* (tampering with decisions inside the task) rather than instruction override. a7Rw7sWq9e (ActionWhisper) hijacks voice agents' tool calls with adversarial audio. On the defense side, 2027 favours formal or quantitative execution control: shield synthesis (gJGOnXZUG0), proof-carrying commit gates (EQzds0sfQc), risk-budget access control (lK03wKqHMk) and least-privilege benchmarks (k0wjCXb6ut).
- **AI control / scheming threat models get concrete in 2027.** E5jqE2d3TR spreads attack fragments (weight exfiltration, rogue deployment) across several agents so that per-trajectory monitors miss them; suspicion falls from 9.0 to 1.5. ngsSCsHa1r transfers deployment-vs-evaluation steering vectors from already-deployed models to counter evaluation awareness. ap1V0VNd3D (KnownLieBench) verifies what the agent knows before scoring deception. The 2026 counterpart is CoT monitorability via information theory (6whdepL0v1).
- **Methodological self-critique and measurement validity are a clear 2027 trend.** Examples: safety datasets over-rely on trigger words (SeyVLPRqw1, "intent laundering"); prompt-injection judges react to the "ignore previous instructions" announcement rather than the attack (bV8443VniY); model self-reports of their own behaviour are generic (oNQG0QrCdV); AI-generated-video detectors learn motion shortcuts (H4uJMqNHtX); zero-shot text detectors are dominated by decoding settings (dPWpXPLvQS); reward models that score well statically get hacked under optimisation (MjGapmZbid, reward-fidelity inversion). Many 2027 abstracts use preregistration, matched-twin or controlled designs (0yDuLVVVWn, Cdps3inyXC).
- **Reward hacking studied with exact oracles.** guoqZlJpx3 compiles authorization policies into exact process rewards and degrades the verifier along 7 axes. In 2027, reward-hacking framing also spreads to video and T2I RL (exXUns94vF, 0HuBR9Qqvq) and to medical VLM DPO (GZUtb8xWwd).
- **Rare-event safety estimation becomes its own topic.** papImkPLf5 (2026) gives PAC bounds on harmful-output probability; 2027 follows with ALE (2ortwDBy1P), which uses the fine-tuning delta as an amplification direction to estimate rare failure rates. Related rigour in 2027: undetectable PRC watermarks become practical (MWiEn9GGuT, ~450 tokens), and probabilistic guarantees for recursive self-improvement (wV4bK1dHpC).
- **New modalities and domains.** These include: omni-model cross-modal safety probes (HpFFDlV2r1); compositional harm in image-editing models (jMxBxR3yle); JPEG compression used as a VLM/VLA attack trigger (bgjT8XlYOl); differentially private VLA fine-tuning (SFMr0umHED); safe exploration for VLA online RL (dcB4KFUuse, LIBERO-Safety); watermarking for diffusion LMs and audio (wPnu90ms5m, DluPNPqS2E); and hallucination detection for masked-diffusion VLMs (vu0ApHXTM6, LLaDA-V). Population-specific safety benchmarks also appear: Chinese adolescents (3VCZJn8bF6), Machiavellian advice (BoyoTNQiGf, 48LawsBench), and C2C agent marketplaces (ondeWR9g8k).
- **Recurring artifacts.** Benchmarks: TOFU, InjecAgent, Terminal-Bench, tau2-bench, LIBERO, ASVspoof 5, MIMIC, CIFAR/ResNet. Models: Qwen3 / Qwen2.5 and Llama-3.1 are the default open models; many safety-area papers that are not interpretability papers still use residual-stream probing or steering (refusal-direction analyses in YVEKBwggSO and vKHCM9O25U).
