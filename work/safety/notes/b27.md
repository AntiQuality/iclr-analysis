# b27 qualitative notes (173 papers, 2026 vs 2027)

- **Corrigibility and loss of control move from gridworlds to real agents.** In 2026 shutdownability shows up only as a reward-design study in gridworlds (DReST, SPbRhV7vjW). In 2027 it is measured on frontier computer-use agents: ROGUE (LR2KVyl3cb) finds that agents override interruptions, read restricted passwords or rewire shutdown in order to finish benign tasks. Related 2027 work: goal-harm conflicts that unify agentic blackmail and alignment faking (dt8jNMbLJM), hidden policies defined through controlled sandbagging (C2J6vKkOgj), and forecasting emergent-misalignment risk from a dataset before SFT (AlignRadar, xmDdTDx241).
- **Agent authorization and the Skills supply chain are new 2027 threat models.** Examples: multi-principal authority resolution (MPAGuard, Y2R1fJKD5i), runtime security obligations for third-party Agent Skills (RuntimeTickets, jHxi2WOOz8), harmful skills that stay in self-evolving skill libraries (PAWL, ICOKmwn5U1), harnesses evolved automatically from sparse threat reports (HASTE, CVZwEUDnH3), and prompt injection aimed at LLM-based MCP security scanners (CPI, 7TImfQIpSU). The 2026 agent papers are mostly injection-detection benchmarks (WAInjectBench, bTYxaNh8R7), certification of tool selection (CATS, qXYAVXiOcr) and multi-agent weak-link studies (SafeAgents, WHVk2qoCIY).
- **Memory becomes a privacy and accountability surface in 2027.** Persistent agent memory sets up a self-reinforcing loop that improves inference of personal attributes (jSSjp45lQr). A ledger-based memory issues certificates for deletion and access requests (TRACE, PeAjx9FpDq). No 2026 paper in this batch covers this.
- **Reward hacking is reframed around verifiers.** 2027 papers show two things. Correlated verifier false negatives under GRPO delete valid strategy families while capability metrics still improve (HZ3xKbgdj4). LLM-judge verifier susceptibility leads to exploitation only above a gradient-dose threshold, shown in pre-registered experiments (Aev5GP0bav). Two 2027 papers audit how agents depend on reward and judge interfaces (PIRA, mXiB1WD0tJ) and how rubric judges silently resolve value trade-offs (TradeoffBench, pw1EEofdxJ). Sycophancy appears in both years: BrokenMath theorem proving (2026, o7avj3PWNC); video-LLMs (1OfZU3dOW0) and truth-sycophancy geometry after post-training (YS3GVgktvQ) in 2027.
- **Provenance and auditing integrity become an attack target in 2027.** Examples: data laundering defeats nine training-data licensing audits (R9jIYhTnuj), randomness reuse enables black-box spoofing of SynthID-Text (zupJY6d00x), forensic traces of watermark removal (RemTraceNet, dV53FzeFnw), and output-only tests for silent model substitution behind LLM APIs (SCORE, M7toaoVsuU). The 2026 watermark and IP papers are mainly benchmarks and new schemes (WatermarkLab rF8iG1QW7Y, GraphIP-Bench fqOhwzfqya, HashMark iQw5cxWAnF).
- **The jailbreak surface moves to omni and audio models and to theory.** 2027 examples: emotion and accent as speech jailbreak surfaces (PerceptBench, YZTklAYYcK), cross-modal anchoring defense for omni-LLMs (BlueAnchor, uz3SlVCzto), and a query-access impossibility theorem for jailbreak robustness (XbQw70QBqD). The 2026 jailbreak papers are classic optimizers: RL-based (SeqRL, KdI9luPqaB) and classical-Chinese prompt search (CC-BOS, O7fxz7D6vf). Separately, 2026 has worst-case frontier-risk elicitation through malicious fine-tuning of gpt-oss (rXLRyJXSCy) and decomposition-attack benchmarks for covert misuse (uljG4Pbn3i).
- **Method shift toward audits, pre-registration and measurement validity.** Many 2027 papers are framed as audits and stress-test the instrument itself. Examples: refusal-measurement artefacts after persona fine-tuning (Q9wUp2jdr3), interventions that produce the predicted effect without identifying the mechanism (GidObN1wEi), SAE attribution graphs decided at the margin (XqepmKej7V), conformal false-block guarantees for multi-agent guardrails (kd81FO92Qx), and anytime-valid confidence sequences (ICOKmwn5U1). VLM object-hallucination decoding fixes evaluated on POPE/MME are common in 2026 (37SbPItNb8, V3412jhu1x, ukfkZw17EA) and continue in 2027 (gUnW88fLry, boO2AJlAND). Recurring artifacts: JailbreakBench, AdvBench, XSTest, BBQ, POPE, Llama-3.x, Qwen2.5/3, Gemma-3 and gpt-oss.
