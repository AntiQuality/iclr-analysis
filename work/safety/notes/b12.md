# b12 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent attack surface moves from "web page text" to the harness itself (2027).** New threat models target the permission and execution layer: privilege escalation through agent-proposed allow-rules in coding harnesses (TO4R4c6u7V), over-privileged Android permission grants by GUI agents (F3ja1kB3PD), malicious SKILL.md agent skills at ecosystem scale (Uv5GeVUEkP), injection carriers in machine-readable DOM, parser output or runtime content (wnFuR14QBF), and topology-aware multi-point injection in multi-agent systems (GliI8fGM76). The 2026 agent papers in this batch are mostly red-teaming of search-augmented LLMs (GMDzJdJPik).
- **PI defenses in 2027 rely on authorization and provenance rather than pattern detection.** Examples: an execution-boundary authorization contract (VWqcwAURmD, APEX), delegation-scoped provenance plus DelegationBench (CA1JtSYaeg), provenance-aware authorization (MCAAG in wnFuR14QBF), and community-level privacy and authorization enforcement (4gCzAMayy0, CoSec). Probing hidden states before the action also recurs as a detector (yoJP018EVw for IPI on GLM-5.2 and Kimi-K3; Uv5GeVUEkP). Benchmarks that keep appearing: AgentDojo and AgentDyn.
- **Reward hacking is now a measurement and validity problem for agents.** 2027 papers audit benchmark shortcuts (LBOKEpNNaD, HackDetect, 11 agent benchmarks), find that production coding agents (claude-opus-4.7, gpt-5.5) "build to the test" (gCG1W765UW), run dose-response studies of flawed verifiers in synthetic agentic RL environments (RWjv2Zg0Wa: 16.8% of Agent World Model verifiers pass a no-op), monitor self-evolving loops (c3uJYTRld6), and aggregate reward models at inference time (drWyIP55rJ). The 2026 reward-hacking papers here are mainly about T2I diffusion (uEgKiy3RmP, rUDEPUZAvL).
- **More negative and null results that question evaluation practice (2027).** Introspection measured on 8 open-weight models is at chance (JINWOT9zWh). Harder tasks do not make CoT more monitorable (xIuaaiFQOs). Safety monitors mostly flag requests the model would refuse anyway (75zQg9DH4L). Harm-classifier choice decides which intervention ranks best (TKVMMf0rVm). A trivial shortcut axis reaches 0.84 AUROC in VLM hallucination detection (HGN2jtzKg0). Emergent-misalignment defenses are hard to separate from benign continued training (0yYaGuJaFa). Comparative framing changes social-bias conclusions (ee0bbQDoIf). 2026 papers in this batch are more often new attack or defense methods.
- **Backdoor threat models now involve time, conditions and lifecycle stages.** Triggers activate through system-prompt timestamps (e8w3aO02sq, 2026), structural cardinality (7K1S8y2CXa), benign downstream fine-tuning in agents (YkrfH3jQH9), unlearning requests (6tKMBrSuMW), and sequential SFT-to-DPO/PPO poisoning (ZbDn4z3C2B). Also: runtime backdoor detection for VLA policies (XOZ6lNL12D) and a mechanistic account of subliminal learning (oHOF5q7TfM).
- **Watermarking and provenance spread beyond text and images and get audited in deployment.** Covered: genome-model distillation (nHn3GLsgBS), protein generative models with biosecurity framing (TMZtF7KYkE), split-key public LLM watermark detectors (YzIlhcjUs1), and a black-box audit of the announced SynthID-Text deployment on Claude/Fable (eE4PTEPhPU, inconclusive). 2026 work is mostly image watermarks and removal attacks (1fYQOZovHR, 8p8BFy5oNt, YDaxkBBRB5) and generated-image detection.
- **Embodied and VLA safety becomes a distinct cluster in 2027.** It includes physically realizable adversarial patches on OpenVLA/pi0 with LIBERO (hPdzz302eJ), proactive runtime failure detection (yrtavje95K), VLA backdoors (XOZ6lNL12D), and repair-based defenses for collaborative perception in autonomous driving (3kKbnibuAz).
- **Mechanistic interpretability is widely used as a safety-analysis tool, but much of it is still INTERP_GEN.** Safety-targeted examples: the judgment-action gap in agents (0gjDrA3wA0), MoE expert-routing safety audits (WKAeqAv0JU), and probe-gated defenses. Many 2027 interp papers are general theory on SAEs, crosscoders, circuits or localization (BSDkUPrqAh, silvZVinbm, K4AWuYu6Q3). Models cited in 2027 abstracts include Opus 5 (w0OSBRyfSU on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and OSWorld-Verified), Claude Sonnet 4.6, Qwen3-VL and DeepSeek-V3.
