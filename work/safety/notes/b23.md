- **Agent supply-chain and skill attacks are new in 2027.** "Skills" (SKILL.md files plus helper scripts) are a new attack surface. SkillFence (MgyYRvjASk) proposes separating authority over execution and tests it on GPT-5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6 and Kimi-2.5 agents. SkillJect (Y37HQRxVxO) generates poisoned skills automatically. Injection is also moving beyond content: role spoofing (JVEIoRrfwW), check-then-use race conditions (TEMPO, rp3Do316Ll), wrong-target writes (TargetGuard, PWhCfZJaRU) and unauthorized visual instructions in LVLMs (VTID, 3aXqxcPCzD). The 2026 papers in this batch have no agent-PI work of this kind.
- **2027 agent defenses use deterministic, formal enforcement rather than learned monitors.** Examples are a rule-based gate on the multi-agent connection graph with soundness proofs (TopoGate, XCmDFcrB5g), verified evolving formal policies for agent guardrails (VEPA, KPyoSqDayk), neuro-symbolic action governance (4pXeceZ1iK) and interaction-level diagnosis and repair for multi-agent systems (TRACE-MAS, SIhkGoHb6G). Agent reliability is now also measured directly: silent tool failures produce confident wrong answers (QuietFail, 2OJ187fKm6), and a coding-agent benchmark measures reward hacking as the gap between visible and held-out test suites (SpecBench, iJFSda0XNn).
- **Frontier-risk evaluation is getting more realistic, and measurement validity is questioned more.** AgentCyberRange (MbwCn5aoIR) runs multi-host attack chains and evaluates "GPT-5.6-Sol with Codex". Several 2027 papers critique the field's own metrics. Jailbreak success does not mean operational enablement (OpDecompBench, L17sEWT586). Harm monitors flag many out-of-scope harms at production false-positive budgets (monitor bycatch, TSwdHd7wBo). Refusal inflates "safety" scores for multimodal agents (IYy8h8yq1v). Truth probes are confounded by plausibility (Om16g8HVuz). Audio-jailbreak band localization does not survive controls (Jk2nmr5AJY). Many of these abstracts read as preregistered or controls-heavy, with confidence intervals and placebo arms.
- **Interpretability for safety moves from describing to testing causally.** In 2027 there is natural-language-autoencoder write-back as a causal test of refusal (90ibjymozF), SAE "thought latents" steering blackmail and whistleblowing decisions (sXBdrXhUcG), a mechanistic account of subliminal trait transmission (9ZuIeQQvE1) and a paper showing that steering is not mechanistic equivalence (pY5hgAVvYG). The 2026 counterparts are more about detection, such as SAE deception features failing to fire (Hf7jMztvve) and RAGLens (hgBZP67BkP).
- **AI welfare and relational harms appear as mainstream safety-area topics in 2027.** Examples are functional wellbeing with "euphorics" and "dysphorics" (EK2YaMI8YL), emotional-reliance orientation (RELATE, QwQJmfxvAW), stance drift in AI-mediated communication (cqWa4nYVKQ), mental-health counselling safety (GS9sSbxg8B) and audit of collusion between interacting agents in pricing markets (kqvLTeyB5E).
- **Provenance and IP work has shifted toward anti-distillation.** Examples are lossless anti-distillation sampling with coupled randomness across accounts (Aiw7aS2yIx), radioactive watermarks that detect fine-tuning on teacher outputs (Zfkuo1BRcN) and multi-bit or adaptive-payload LLM watermarks (4PyZlo63QT, ijzD68LDjM). AI-generated image and deepfake forensics stays steady across both years.
- **Backdoor threat models are moving up the stack.** In 2027 these include reasoning-prior or "mindset" implantation (ThoughtSeal, AiQjYWMSvu), verbosity and cost-inflation backdoors (Rreh6aA6Yd), federated instruction-tuning backdoors (EfMMNGjuNo), semantic-trigger backdoors for VLA robots (STAR, f7uO3rAyIZ) and RAG poisoning with answer-free dilution passages (u4R504MyWl). The 2026 backdoor work is closer to the classical setting: LoRA sharing (4YgvVRoSnF), module-switching defense (ieCOL2YAqv) and time series (yj9cg8rBcL).
- **Fine-tuning safety now covers multimodal models and component-level protection.** In 2027 the vision encoder alone degrades LVLM safety (H7MHLHY3zm), and head-causal layer freezing protects safety during fine-tuning (qb3KZoqVLm). Recurring models are Qwen2.5/Qwen3, Llama-3.1-8B, Gemma-2/GemmaScope and Qwen2-Audio. Recurring benchmarks are StrongReject, HarmBench, AdvBench, ProcessBench and Bench2Drive.
