# b57 qualitative notes (173 papers, 2026 vs 2027)

- **Cryptographic private inference becomes a large 2027 cluster.** 10 of the 72 core 2027 papers are PRIVACY, and most of them are about running LLMs or transformers on encrypted inputs: HE-aware fine-tuning that learns iteration counts (HEAT, fArqFprckh), threshold-HE plus secret sharing for decoding with a KV cache (SHIM, QguE2FYSrj), degree-sparsity compression (DegreeSpar, UHFA411YHy), a benchmark of adaptive MPC/HE execution plans (PrivWeave, ApX86hpoYc), and module-LWE outsourcing of genomic MoE experts (CipherGenome, 7KCUTpkPaS). The 2026 privacy papers here are DP-SGD papers, e.g. disparate impact under adaptive clipping (VRFbLr8Uhv). The 2027 DP-SGD follow-up is EquiConfig (9WukHr2fnG).
- **New supply-chain and pipeline attack surfaces appear in 2027.** Examples: inference-time backdoors hidden in Jinja chat templates of GGUF artifacts, which also hijack agent tool use (whcCSVoCWe); dual-key multimodal conjunctive backdoors in third-party LVLM adapters (GenLock, zU9US3qBih); poisoning the OPQ tokenizer fit set of semantic-ID recommenders (hdASJjZsan); visual cues that survive recursive memory consolidation in embodied agents (CueClimb, k4dFvuGPXI); presentation-preference attacks of the generative-engine-optimization kind on LLM search (wqblBBEBca); and RAG certified against a bounded number of authenticated writer principals rather than a bounded number of records (RootCert, Vt5Eqf6BZd).
- **Agent safety moves from benchmarks to runtime authorization and auditing.** 2027: a deterministic reference monitor with epistemic-status promotion (ECC, TEKU3bDzzM); credential release gated on value-lineage plus attested destination (CallWitness, yrqUzEJ7HS); on-demand multi-agent trajectory auditing (IoD-Auditor, 4d2zPx9UGA); paired harmful/benign tasks that show guardrails rely on surface cues (CoPair-Bench, EChjbaBYts); e-value admission tests for self-written guardrail skills (oegeqwodj7). The 2026 agent papers are attack testbeds and propensity benchmarks instead: Terrarium blackboard MAS attacks (bknuCt8MI7), privacy attack/defense search between agents (nz4ZqbrBEi), the Mandela effect in MAS (yIoMqDes7O), and ImpossibleBench test-case exploitation (SeO4vyAj7E).
- **Emergent misalignment shifts from discovery to mechanism and model-organism tooling.** 2026: toxic-persona SAE features (yjrVOxjkDR) and data-attribution filtering (fQvVV6UN4p). 2027: weight-space geometry, where narrowly harmful LoRA fine-tunes share subspaces and are linearly mode-connected, with data provenance a key factor (qbuqW5zMNg). Also 2027: "user message fine-tuning", which implants beliefs more deeply than synthetic document fine-tuning (pUj6uDM1SK, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B); decision-theoretic detection of steganographic CoT (eNSRDD963q); and weak-to-strong students amplifying sycophancy-type teacher biases (BAF of 2.3–2.8x, ihI4ECm6M8).
- **Unlearning and concept erasure are judged adversarially or against calibrated references in 2027.** Examples: neighbourhood-robust unlearning against rephrase and fine-tune attacks on TOFU (hrveinV6zZ); a feature-recovery measure showing unlearned models match retraining only on the surface (03ELNqpOT7); a "woken recall" calibrated class-unlearning audit (un1FlqpdVM); and one relearning attack for both U-Net SD and flow-matching FLUX (CSR, 3HZVibYUZe). In 2026 the papers in this area are mostly new unlearning methods: attention smoothing (sX9HbELwLO), subspace-noise certified unlearning (1N3q0tA7AW), and federated domain unlearning (p5yByOjvXY).
- **2027 sociopolitical harm evaluations compare US and Chinese models.** PoliBench, on authoritarian assistance (WIh2HzWRSj), finds a "strict-yet-porous" pattern: Chinese models refuse more but are broken more often by TAP. PowerBench (p9oKrOb45L) covers 24 models and power-shifting requests, with nationality-dependent help. Further 2027 items: ownership intuitions compared with 108 humans (COAT, 8WZRf2KxYp); multi-turn medical virtual patients that hide risk (REVEAL, BFI85sE3c5); anxiety-inducing health language across 6 languages (CALM, DXGNmcuxJf). The 2026 counterparts are jailbreak and certification framings: JailNewsBench (7dTqUaY2Kl), USB MLLM safety (TlRrhTU7WR), C3LLM risk certification (yt9TW2WtpG), and secondary risks under benign prompts (MvXJ91WzsO).
- **Interpretability-for-safety targets affect and refusal internals in 2027.** Examples: valence geometry coupled to refusal and to multi-turn attack drift (NZybxmI9Ek); AI-centric versus human-centric emotion probes (Q8jyH7NJDn); duplicate-token heads as an exploitable piece of a safety circuit (MC82YzfGkA); predicting false refusal before the response (qr6vL8inMI); causal tracing of indirect prompt injection on AgentDojo (xxyC5WkjEh); and ConflictEval, which tests 144 sequential defense compositions that undo each other (S3yiqSxOXG).
- **Reward hacking and recurring artifacts.** In 2026, reward hacking appears in RLHF (MaxEnt-RL over-optimization, G4CN6xgQ3k; rubric rewards for the high-reward tail, pBjy4ek2QV). In 2027 it moves to verifier and search settings: maximin PRM-guided search (mPtkeRxjEA), auditing agentic-science discovery claims against a fallible verifier (HRp5OacQyC), and specification gaming in molecular generation (M2GuwJn5mO). The 2027 style matches other batches: dense "audit/certificate" papers with preregistered or frozen protocols and confidence intervals. Recurring artifacts: Qwen2.5/Qwen3, Llama, Gemma-2, Pythia, TOFU/MUSE/WMDP, AgentDojo, TAP, POPE, FLUX and Stable Diffusion, Tree-Ring watermarks. 2027 AI-image-detection work audits false positives on real photos that look fake (FLORIDA, mOTHJE79Be).
