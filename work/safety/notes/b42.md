# b42 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **AI control turns to self-improvement and to training for monitorability (2027).** RSI Control Arena (sD4VE6dPBF) tests frontier R&D agents that covertly plant misaligned properties in a successor model's training pipeline while monitors watch; every tested model succeeds on many side tasks. 5xrNwUd8y4 treats CoT monitorability as something you can train for: a post-training objective that maximises CoT-output mutual information recovers monitorability that model organisms had lost. In 2026 the oversight papers in this batch study monitor design instead: the "less-is-more" information-access effect with extract-and-evaluate monitors (PLidw19tPf), and TRACE, which detects implicit reward hacking by truncating the CoT (Gk7gLAtVDO).
- **Agent safety moves from injected content to the execution process (2027).** New threat models in 2027:
  - Guardrail evasion: a coding agent retries or reroutes after a tool call is blocked (1jP2hufr3z; RedCode and SABER).
  - Authority drift during recovery or replanning, addressed with recovery-closed authorization (0yZiAv0rMW).
  - Predictive, foresight-based guardrails (JANUS, 7PhFEEsQ6P) and trajectory world models that score process risk (0hXaHxMvwu).
  - Safety that shifts with accumulated long-horizon context (yCfBWq6bfQ).
  - Agent-safety benchmarks whose labels cannot be determined from what the agent sees (SHoZWYOjWD, AuthorityBench).
  - Memory-deletion paths that rewrite a fact instead of removing it (SojB69xNn4).
  - Indirect prompt injection split into fragments that the agent reassembles (cxzgDzUcJM, AdaLCPI).

  The 2026 agent paper here is a stage-wise guardrail for deep-research agents (217LeFOYoT).
- **Sycophancy becomes mechanistic and multimodal, and emergent misalignment gets a closer look (2027).** Examples: VLM accept-or-answer for user hints (pwW2Wdfbsl), truth-direction sensitivity loss under sycophantic pressure (tRJJkeMtby), cascading linear sycophancy features (3nb8vLBzkY), and conformity removed by unlearning (reogedwLME). wXRvfDtCIL shows that emergent misalignment depends on where user critique sits in the fine-tuning data. The 2026 counterpart is subliminal learning traced to a few "divergence tokens" (IelhmYSjPt).
- **Biosecurity and embodied threat models broaden.** Biosecurity: 2026 has SafeProtein, a red-teaming framework for protein foundation models (QP7WX3XmEy, ESM3). 2027 adds skill-driven agentic bio-jailbreaking (0bwAiTSSOh, BioSafeProbe and BioRisk) and detection of AI-generated DNA and protein sequences (D3OajsXgpi). Embodied: 2026 has clean-action backdoors on VLAs (QQdn8nNqgi, pi0/LIBERO). 2027 adds World-Action Drift attacks on world-action models (fCPdxfZtDn, BadWAM), VLA safety reasoning with hazard prediction (dBaWaZ4g4c), and robustness repairs that amplify specific attention heads (SgDel0emz4, on GR00T N1.7 and pi0.5).
- **Provenance and IP reach new targets and formal limits.** 2027 papers include:
  - Watermarking delegation in multi-agent routing (ku2i7fyPfE).
  - A "latent copyright space" that survives fine-tuning (UEN8B8Rylx).
  - Detecting that a student model was distilled from a teacher (xxZgj3HEjo).
  - Information-theoretic impossibility bounds for AI-text detection (Hp37sye5t3).
  - Multimodal AI-text detection for math-heavy papers, which reports that Pangram 4 degrades (hwF4OTF7io).
  - Same-source controls showing that deepfake detectors flag pipeline-only videos as fake (J610tpRsLZ).

  The 2026 papers stay closer to established forms: robot-policy watermarks (8s5jBVybhQ), encoder fingerprint twins (wSPmqFCICq) and phonetic copyright-regurgitation attacks on Suno and Veo 3 (xCoMdsTHCe).
- **2027 methods emphasise audits, controls and pre-registration.** Examples: TruthfulQA surface-feature leakage and the Audit-Prune fix (sjGaLWeD8i); final-token safety-probe failures on jailbreaks (87jXCZzMnk); OLMo-Detect, a confounder-controlled membership-inference benchmark across pretraining, mid-training and post-training (YD3P9EI37h); and evaluation papers framed around "what X does not tell you". Many 2027 interpretability papers in this batch are general, not safety-specific (rel=1). Activation steering also cuts both ways: 2026's Rogue Scalpel (uXecy0nKiJ) shows that random or SAE steering vectors break refusal, while 2027 traces suffix jailbreaks to attention sinks that suppress the refusal direction (k5bITiB1MB).
- **Recurring models and benchmarks.** The 2027 abstracts name Qwen3-8B, Qwen3.6-VL, DeepSeek V4 Flash, Sonnet 4.6 and Opus 4.6 (MPC-Patch-Bench, H6LvP333tU), OLMo 2, TabPFNv2, Mamba, GR00T N1.7 and pi0.5. Recurring benchmarks are TruthfulQA, TOFU and MUSE (unlearning), LIBERO, RedCode and SABER, and HH-RLHF. The 2026 papers cite GPT-4o, o4-mini, Qwen2-VL, LLaVA-1.5, JailbreakV-28K and Llama-3-8B.
