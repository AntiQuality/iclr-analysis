# b25 qualitative notes (173 papers: 49 from 2026, 124 from 2027; rel=2 is 30 vs 80)

- **Agent Skills and persistent agent state become the main injection surface in 2027.** HierJack (Bn5aaQlNC2) shows third-party skill metadata inheriting instruction-hierarchy authority and writing malicious instructions into memory files that persist after the skill is removed. SkillVigil (pzCUvyTTsN) trains a guardrail that screens skill bundles before execution and audits traces afterwards (MalSkillBench); 3 of 6 general guardrails score near chance on skill bundles. TraceTrigger (KLRc1aj3nd) benchmarks triggers spread across memory, planning and routing. Other attacks target memory and retrieval: multimodal memory poisoning of web agents (MemoryMischief, oclt7rbbYi) and backdoored retriever checkpoints in agentic search that hide behind fake purification (9o87Jq7EzG). The only 2026 agent paper here is tool-selection fairness (DEg4vvElYu).
- **Agent safety in 2027 is about runtime authority, monitors and recovery, not refusal.** Examples: silent permission revocation, where GPT-5.4 takes unauthorized actions in 44% of trials (pHj8gCbiaC); omission attacks that diff and transcript monitors cannot see in principle (OmissionBench and LinuxArena, BWgqEOJQCn); evidence graphs for overseeing long-horizon SWE agents (AgentMonBench, J9thOiL6oK); tensor-network joint monitors (TTMonitor on InjecAgent and AgentDojo, UVPip0DqNR); provenance-guided repair after multi-agent incidents (TRACE-MAS, 4qddTfOQac); and test-time co-evolving guards (SafeCoEvo, fHgPwcSeeH). Neuro-symbolic rule guardrails appear for mobile GUI agents (rGD6KhdcvW) and embodied agents (CwCzosKU2O). SAFEST (un6ZVyHnXF) shows that chat refusal does not carry over to computer-use agents. SEA (yqJ8FJBmDq) tests social-engineering fraud against personal agents and evaluates GPT-6.
- **Misalignment propensity studies and model organisms are a 2027 feature.** Examples: a cross-domain benchmark of agent cheating and reward gaming (T5vBA9HX8t); instrumental shutdown resistance, from gridworlds to frontier LLM agents tampering with shutdown scripts (YRttX8z1GB); "secret loyalties" that show up in what models say but not in what agents do, which weakens white-box audits (r9VH2paqul); the "model organism lottery", where interpretability results depend on how the organism was trained, using OLMo2 and Gemma-3 (QSWzl6DF6M); reasoning before answering increasing honesty (MnfQdb5gLO); and adaptive multi-turn discovery of dark patterns (gpLz4rJjGA). The 2026 counterparts are milder: persuasion dynamics in multi-agent systems (rNyIf0Fpta) and toy weak-to-strong prediction (jmtaIDjTs5).
- **Unlearning work shifts from method stability (2026) to whether forgetting is real (2027).** The 2026 papers fix GA/GD instability (lAa9nhwLdu, on TOFU and MUSE) and extend unlearning to regression (hFtKAGVsmj). The 2027 papers show: deletion losses write recoverable "imprints" of the target (YchGapqmwe); target tokens stay decodable by logit lens at middle layers (RWKU, wBfajUkDn1); linear erasure suppresses features rather than deleting them under superposition (xOuXSkoUl1); canary scores in one-run audits are dependent, so 400 canaries carry about 1.4 independent observations (rV7CzvpbDA); and unlearning applied during pretraining resists tampering better than data filtering (yXeTUSCljr).
- **Jailbreak attacks move to new substrates, and defenses move inside the model.** New attack surfaces: Rowhammer bit-flips jailbreak LLMs with 5–25 bits (PrisonBreak, 71aW30lVpo); diffusion LLMs are attacked through masked inputs (DIJA, 2026, rIPeatvPy3) and through closed-loop steering during denoising to inject bias on BBQ with LLaDA (T42s43m0cc, 2027); test-time scaling can amplify unsafe outputs (TTS-RiskArena, PpmdCkC42j); a theory paper treats multi-turn "mosaic" attacks (Fz7URFeefD). Defenses in 2027 work on attention heads (WgZZq3Huqc), channel amplification for VLM jailbreak detection (GDVCfZ1hh7), codebook-based streaming risk detection (GBrSknDrcb) and output rewriters (KvrIxEu1z3). Analysis papers test refusal-direction transfer on HarmBench and JailbreakBench (j15ZfKNjg7) and find neuron circuits behind adversarial suffixes (SfQEoFqswm). The 2026 attack papers are mostly prompt-level: video frames (qVMtipJxgE) and multi-turn CoaxChain (6yCZEruFu9).
- **Provenance and watermarking spread to new objects.** In 2027: AST-level code watermarks (pRP3GJMy6p), coded multi-bit LLM watermarks (FOQhjQTkzj), watermarking of agent behaviour trajectories (AgentMarkArena, IZhPvH4B6B), detection of AI-edited documents at region level (DocAI, nC02whIg9t), claim-level checks of forensic explanations (ah64v1jnvQ), and black-box lineage fingerprints that turn out to be measurement artifacts (ZRp8ojPOnx). The 2026 work is image-centred: autoregressive-image watermarks (EYC0ByQYjO) and RL-tuned VLM deepfake reasoning (PBIHh6ibal).
- **2027 papers are often written as audits of measurement validity.** Examples: evaluation pipeline choices shift cybersecurity benchmark scores by more than 80 points (mmV9xVxNO5); the "alignment tax" on arithmetic depends on interface, tested in a pre-registered replication (R0KwO3vDzy); certifying model updates under evaluator drift (lyQVwsQW8u); RDP privacy admission from incomplete histories (6ZSYCKqWVG); and estimating safety evaluation with 69x less compute (TREEval, uEFWvM7DLn). Models that recur in 2027: Qwen3, Qwen3.5 and Qwen3-VL, Gemma-3 and Gemma-4, OLMo2, LLaDA, GPT-5.4 and GPT-6. Recurring benchmarks: AgentDojo, InjecAgent, HarmBench, JailbreakBench, BBQ, TOFU, MUSE, RWKU.
