# b32 notes (173 papers; mostly 2027, ~30 from 2026)

- **Emergent misalignment (EM) is now a whole research line in 2027, not a single finding.** Papers look at EM arising from routine sources: benign training data that passes safety filters (1Azj6zZhg5, LMSYS/Dolci, 15.95% EM rate), activation steering vectors (Y6BZ2vuP69), and a weaker inoculation-prompting effect once models know about inoculation prompting (4Mg0HWnNSL, Llama-3.3-70B). None of the 2026 papers in this batch use this framing.
- **Agents that change themselves and keep state are a new threat surface in 2027.** Papers cover misalignment from self-evolving agent harnesses (X0eCGaNLsC SEABench), whether edits to agent skill libraries are validated in context (ml4xqcOZny), contaminated or stale persistent memory and provenance-gated promotion of memory (Lupg86mC2r, MsX014tAvl), integrity violations by AI-scientist agents (842NHIqIg4 RIDAR), and agents that use their own unsupported claims to justify unsafe actions (h524snJ5zS). The 2026 agent papers in this batch focus on per-turn guardrails instead (qRGKHBE5RY, mPh4B1JoAN).
- **Prompt injection has moved beyond web pages.** New 2027 vectors include provider-side injection in proactive recommender agents (JolPRdtcdd), delayed multi-page injection in web agents (hiX9SvzDyw STRAP), visual patches that hijack mobile GUI agents (6wPBIkuLSw), DoS by truncating tool responses through a marketplace plugin (BcztkwoCKv), third-party social influence on SWE-bench agents (4tq4GJuZVx), and MCP shared-workspace benchmarks that name Opus 5 / Sonnet 5 as the most robust models (g1rzEH4l9g APEX-Harm). There is also LLM-as-security-tool evasion of vulnerability and malware detectors (LePzIv8HAX, SG88ZsW9LD).
- **Oversight and monitorability research now covers diffusion LMs and other new channels.** Examples: the first sandbagging and secret-keeping model organisms in diffusion LMs, testing whether a monitor can read the draft canvas (1ueGhmCmE1), extraction of alignment data from masked diffusion LMs (UUcPqCJLiM), an introspective "observer channel" trained for oversight on Qwen3.6-27B (M7bhrIY7b9), monitors for covert agent sabotage based on attribution graphs versus probes (LHpYWmI7nr), CoT "bypass" faithfulness in distilled reasoning models (3bKNdyeq05), and debate robustness to "persuasion by echo" (bP1FVIXIr7).
- **2027 papers lean heavily on methodology: pre-registration, confound controls and negative results.** Many audit an earlier method rather than propose a new one. Examples: confounds in steering measurement (MqAq77hklK), calibration of refusal-direction anchoring during fine-tuning (7l9gH9h9aZ), split-half reliability of fractal AI-text detectors (k09CaBYk9E), pre-registered cheap-talk honesty benchmark (07HyQCqbme), and pre-registered skill validation (ml4xqcOZny). The 2026 papers here are mostly conventional method-plus-SOTA papers (DP theory, adversarial robustness, DPO variants).
- **Multimodal safety has spread to new settings.** Examples: audio-LM jailbreak benchmark JALMBench (DJkQ236C8B, 2026), egocentric assistants with harm-target-aware refusal (7qvCeMdFpT STOU), voice agents acting on bystander speech (yn7NRfRwdr), synergistic harmful image-text generation in unified MLLMs (9RPk7sxpaK), low-resource languages Tibetan and Cantonese where capability failure looks like safety (4fEnZd9kF4), and hallucination in omni-modal and multi-image settings (oOFndOXCUT, l4YckUQepC).
- **Recurring models and benchmarks:** Qwen2.5/Qwen3 (including Qwen3-8B and Qwen3-VL-235B), Llama-3/3.3, Gemma-3, LLaDA/Dream diffusion LMs, GCG/AutoDAN/HarmBench, Agent-SafetyBench, SWE-bench, WebVoyager, TOFU, FLUX.1/SD3. Frontier models named in 2027 papers: GPT-5.6, Opus 5, Sonnet 5.
- **Steady background areas:** DP/federated LoRA and privacy theory (FfmlgiKZCc, vDDJLIR9xO, NUkP7guv48, seU1LYAiwm optimizer-state leakage), multi-bit and paraphrase-robust LLM watermarks (YPsRQL9gyI, ga85Tp54Ua, Hy2ZG4IgBD), and AI-generated image detection with MLLMs (FwzDg8qGWN, ittJU5NZcd, Zp66glOivu).
