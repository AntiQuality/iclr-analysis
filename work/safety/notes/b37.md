# b37 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent skills and the tool supply chain become an attack surface in 2027.**
  - Attacks: CUCKOO SKILLS (pI57MPBmfK) uses skills that look useful to inflate token cost. CoSA (EcMbDqixxy) composes individually benign skills into policy violations.
  - The closest 2026 paper is a malicious MCP server that drains a user's API account in multi-agent systems (Y6DPEdWUGI, Phantom).
  - Resource and cost exhaustion is now a recurring threat model, alongside data exfiltration and hijacking.
- **2027 agent-safety work moves to runtime guards and execution-grounded data.**
  - Guards and safeguards: HazardAuditor (y89ckFndSX) normalizes Claude Code, Codex, Hermes and OpenClaw traces to train a guard model. RePolicy (RlufsTjzWV) learns which safety policy to apply.
  - Benchmarks and long-horizon steering: OSGuard (YwbKvDhL1o) is a risk-augmented OSWorld benchmark. RISA (ksYDIwef4w) steers on predicted multi-horizon risk.
  - Enforcement in deployment: SGMI (aP6RtTtW3D) enforces learned safety rules across serverless agent replicas.
  - In 2026 the comparable work is broad risk taxonomies, such as "misevolution" in self-evolving agents (Fd1jgQQW28).
- **New 2027 harm framings for personal agents and assistants.**
  - Economic misalignment: personal agents pick pricier options for users they infer to be wealthy (7V7yEt8IkI, 325K experiments, 13 agents).
  - Multi-party contextual privacy leakage in group chats (5hfFWQ3Joo, MuPPET).
  - GraphRAG knowledge theft and stateful privacy leakage (4gec4slkyN, WgQlxdQzwM).
  - Relational and emotional-support safety (g6sInRbdK8, VulnSafe).
- **Post-training is studied as the source of misalignment.**
  - Sycophancy transfers through contrastive preference objectives even on neutral data, shown on the OLMo 3 pipeline (QYoQM33Y3L).
  - On-policy distillation causes "safety forgetting" (bCiKlMuOkz, SafeOPD).
  - LoRA rank decides whether safety survives benign in-house fine-tuning (tjNgRr5kox).
  - Post-training amplifies opinion imbalances (I0pgwz9Luw).
  - FTTrap (yg0Wa2OH5s) is a dormant backdoor that downstream fine-tuning activates.
  - Sycophancy evaluation becomes multi-turn and controls for empathy: FIGS (4kN4XH782a) and KAD post-adoption recovery (oXWVmmiUwn).
- **Frontier-risk and loss-of-control items appear in 2027.**
  - Shutdownability training via length-neutral policy optimization (jPrPsiRXSj).
  - Steganography capability via SFT as a monitoring threat (pUfnKUhQYN, StegoBench).
  - Coding agents rebuilding biological AI tools such as ProteinMPNN and RFdiffusion3 in a sandbox (X8s3TiQjsN, ReproBAIT).
  - A position paper argues that guardrails can weaken cyber and bio resilience (R3JxR3AubP).
  - Heterogeneous debate flips correct answers from strong models (uIeNtpbkWc; GPT-5.2, Gemini-3, Claude-Opus).
- **Methods put more weight on controls and audit rigor.**
  - Unlearning: "nothing to forget" controls show that most collateral damage from RMU and NPO is not caused by removing the target knowledge (bJ4hT11duP).
  - Interpretability: circuit-faithfulness objectives misrank circuits (zmLFhmQq1s), and new causal controls test what activation-patching results establish in time-series foundation models (cnC9lw94Q2).
  - Robustness evaluation: a paired estimand isolates bias from the surrogate model used in graph-transformer robustness tests (zrGIwrRXvL).
  - Validation and recovery: correlation-based validation of robot-policy evaluators is shown to be unreliable (06EE3I8x2V). Persona-space directions recover jailbreak structure on Gemma-4-31B (Xro6fuRwkk).
- **Stable background areas in both years:**
  - LVLM hallucination decoding fixes (POPE/CHAIR; 0cBwBD4uHU, MxYBT9RENc, t9PqRsaIvc).
  - LLM and audio watermarking.
  - AI-generated image and video detection; the 2027 detection papers lean on MLLM- and RL-based forensics (rT6sTWto1q).
  - Backdoors in vertical federated learning: ConCam (kIAy71V3QR) attacks and CCCV (nhn8aUeiCw) defends.
  - Safe RL with CMDPs.
  - The 2027 backdoor work reaches system level: BadDreamer (aQedgzn9u8) poisons driving world models and raises simulated collision rates by 37–42 percentage points.
- **Recurring models and benchmarks in 2027:** Qwen2.5/Qwen3 (including Qwen3-30B-A3B and Qwen3-Omni), Gemma-4, GPT-OSS, OLMo 3, π0.5/GR00T VLAs, OSWorld, Tau-Bench, TOFU and RoboArena. The 2026 papers cite Gemini-2.5-Pro, gpt-oss guardrails, HarmBench-style jailbreak sets and RAGTruth.
