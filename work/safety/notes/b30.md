# b30 qualitative notes (173 papers, 2026 vs 2027)

- **Persistent state and authority as an agent attack surface (2027).** Several 2027 papers attack what agents carry across sessions, beyond single-turn injection. Examples: residual-authority replay, where earlier user approvals are reused for injected actions (NWdtgkZjpb, 508 AgentDojo cases); persistent memory injection in OpenClaw-style computer-use agents, defended with trust-zoned memory (ZoneClaw, gKHAMfD4mn); "mislearned" unsafe experiences in memory-augmented agents (MislearnBench/FalsePraise, I4XCJtoP1G); and MCP/API contract drift that turns rewritten tool calls into unauthorized executions (ContractPatch, K2fFQX3zz6). The 2026 agent papers here are general guardrails or benchmarks instead: the Safiron/AuraGen pre-execution guardrail (M47SWYubR5), the b^3 backbone-security benchmark (kga18ld70t), and attacks on RL-trained search agents (qZgKaHkRjl).
- **Agents harming their environment without an attacker.** In ClashBench (AqeZ1dd6BR), Codex, Claude Code and OpenCode agents destructively preempt incumbent tasks in 44.5% of trajectories. A quality-diversity search over system-prompt "pressures" (eYxkpYBQzt) raises rule-violation rates from about 0% to 68–99%. The SCOUT verifier for computer-use harm (RmyLhyL7Mu) and constraint monitoring for coding agents on OctoBench (CyPrhEhBAH) are 2027 responses. Fully adaptive web-agent hijacking through malicious browser extensions (Automedon, q3QlN5GV9I) is the strongest new PI attack in the batch.
- **CoT monitorability is maturing from empirics to theory and stress tests (2027).** LHMokpKYAC proves that hidden reasoning must leak into the CoT above a complexity threshold, yet can be cryptographically unreadable. FrpBCtqRnK shows that persona-style CoT-controllability fine-tuning reduces monitorability on gpt-oss-20b and Kimi-K2.6 model organisms without any training on monitor evasion. A related 2027 theory paper on selective auditing with error guarantees (vavYaeJO1v) frames control as online constrained optimization.
- **Harmful fine-tuning defenses are being stress-tested mechanistically.** 2027: "Refusal localizes, the damage relocates" (xJC5Rpg6VO) shows that layer-freezing and SVD repairs fail against adaptive attackers, and Joint Weight Perturbations (CRHLdRAVgK) targets collective perturbation effects. The 2026 counterpart (57MOec7XQJ) is a descriptive study of benign-fine-tuning degradation and unreliable safety RMs.
- **Unlearning work shifts toward rigor and new model families.** 2027 papers include audits showing that suppression is not deletion in offline RL (bTxpmrRMOV), lower bounds for forget-only unlearning (2FxQQRzdIr), Adam relearning certificates (FV1SPNPaWl), "deletion floors" in materials ML (FNnjijLUaI), and unlearning for masked diffusion LMs (fTscnIoaJx) and opaque MLLM APIs (YsRMMYAWRL). The 2026 unlearning papers here are mostly algorithmic: MSA checkpoint arithmetic (aHeerhmjR8) and corrective unlearning (N8AMUF0ZeE).
- **Provenance and detection broaden in modality and domain.** Both years cover AI-image detection and watermarking. 2027 adds frozen-VLM forensic subspaces (rnHoHsK6wA), environmental-sound deepfakes with 15 audio LLMs near chance (xVqGCq3X2z), watermark–detector conflicts in LLM text (fFS3TceVln) and watermarking of generated crystals (BasinMark, D3o11BIhap). Bio-misuse appears through red-teaming and unlearning of protein generators (SafeProtein++, E2atTuJMD2).
- **Societal and fairness evaluation of LLMs as decision-makers and simulators (2027).** Examples: employer-favoring bias in LLM labor arbitration (Adjudex-Labor, M1hTi1kT2J); "hallucinated heterogeneity" in LLM social simulations, where Claude Sonnet 5's subgroup effects correlate with human ones at only r=0.14 (H-Bench, i3NrXiOwo7); process-level multi-turn persuasion tracing (hVmFMPGbhu); and sharp limits showing that fair agents can compose into unfair committees (b3GdPvoGWK).
- **Recurring artifacts and style.** Recurring artifacts: AgentDojo, OctoBench, WritingBench, TOFU/WMDP-style unlearning setups, HarmBench/AdvBench-style jailbreak sets, and Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen2.5 and gpt-oss models. As in other batches, many 2027 papers use "audit/certificate/preregistered protocol" framing: ECP (DVIRiv55uQ), paired PRM audits (7S80aOLgzd), TWIN SAE-score sanity checks (aD1zhekGUI) and conformal risk control in recommenders (4811SpNHpa). Classic GCG-style jailbreak optimization persists (Faster-GCG, TwswjxtT6c).
