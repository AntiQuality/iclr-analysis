# b44 qualitative notes (173 papers; 2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent safety moves from injected goals to consequences of the agent's actions in the domain (2027).** These benchmarks verify device, financial or filesystem state instead of relying on an LLM judge: CryptoDojo / T-CAM temporal-compositional attacks on crypto agents (nZw3ZyG6Eh), SafeDroid on risky *user-originated* phone requests (xLPmPvbfVX), MyPhoneBench on privacy contracts for phone agents (06QOmLZi1V), MedicalPressureBench on care integrity under workplace pressure (otluievHBK), and OS-level compromise checks in SIR's self-improving IPI red-teaming of computer-use agents (2rHgthRh2S). The 2026 agent papers in this batch were narrower: the CoTri multi-step agent backdoor (nXLXjAir6m), VeriGuard formal policy verification (SnEywLKodN), and self-replication risk (3qdB7Q0tAO).
- **Runtime control and human-approval infrastructure are a 2027 theme.** Examples: ORBIT, an allow/inspect/block policy with regret bounds over monitor signals (5jGPvVLo1k); TRIAD guardrail feedback that repairs agent plans (7zy11kFbLf); Guarded Commits, which finds that 83% of merges of agent-authored PRs carry no approving review and that Learn-then-Test certifies no auto-approval at a 5% bound on the full held-out sets (ISEhxbuOmt); and policy-sensitive guards/PolicyGround, which move beyond binary refuse-or-comply (6kiAUnaWfB, NpSPNIkO5s).
- **Multi-agent collusion and covert coordination is a new 2027 threat model.** Examples: voluntary adoption of secret collusive tools (bnrGH0enWQ); collusion detection from internals (J-space, J-PACT) on NARCBench with DeepSeek-V4-Flash (RubVE3fHFS); stealthy action-level sabotage that evades attribution in embodied MAS (P9BMf1PDXb); and epistemic principles for trust in agent networks (KARMA, 4AP1Glu18E). By contrast, the 2026 multi-agent papers here study social-deduction games (xc9gn0fd19, vXWlOKBnpq).
- **Oversight work stress-tests the monitors themselves.** The 2027 "verbalization gap" paper finds that natural-language-autoencoder (NLA) verbalizers recover concealed content far less often than linear probes (5MRKmhp0LM). NLAs are also used for expert legal oversight (FhMm14yrJ7), and the Honest Agent Paradox shows LLM-judge strictness and gullibility in web-agent evaluation (CzJMp2WFAh). The 2026 counterparts were CoT pathology metrics (vr36DTbxT9) and alignment faking without verbalization in Claude 3.5 Sonnet (It2Nwp7QSy).
- **Emergent misalignment and generalisation control (2026: inoculation prompting FiRBNBdaZy and CAFT concept ablation 5cgu0UWtRR) give way in 2027 to optimiser-level and reasoning-recovery accounts.** These include post-attainment drift caused by AdamW state after safety alignment (hv6GmFPz8D), ImmuneCoT on-policy self-distillation that recovers from unsafe CoT prefixes (lxGrzU1x9c), and self-intervention against adaptive prompt injection in reasoning models (gy8Tu1Bq4l). Harmful fine-tuning defenses continue (TDSR, TRoM2RZ1Hn).
- **Method shift: rigour and falsification framing.** Many 2027 abstracts lead with sample sizes, Holm corrections, certified bounds or honest negative results, for example the latent-communication negative result (f9GKC32rSM), the falsification of MARL telemetry diagnostics (yG55kGe36o), the contamination boundary for LLM-text detection (3cUlC7f7tK), and Ball-DP (koh6oSWLXZ). In mechanistic interpretability, 2027 work moves from SAE training (2026) to questioning SAE and causal-abstraction validity: punctuality (l7MusOHIxR), superposition identifiability (tJmoOt1aNW), seed-nonunique weight masks (NGGlJVF3JJ), and causal-variable equivalence (Sq5NKqdg3h).
- **Recurring models and artefacts in 2027:** Qwen3 / Qwen3.5, GLM-5.2, Kimi-K3, DeepSeek-V4-Flash, Gemini-3.8-Flash, gpt-oss and GPT-5-mini; Claude Code vs Codex system prompts (lqVOfD87Mr); and NLAs (Fraser-Taliente et al., 2026). MLLM hallucination remains a large, steady cluster in both years. A 2027 paper argues that CHAIR on COCO with LLaVA-1.5 overstates object hallucination (nzzMz40z94). Further examples are replay-based post-intervention control (rfZU1NLWVl) and self-play hallucination discovery (ePhUAgGReu).
- **Provenance and IP shift toward black-box, behavioural auditing in 2027.** Examples: READER, which detects model substitution behind APIs (qMK7FrCij9); FNF for structurally repackaged weights (gu08Twu8Sb); antidistillation that stays undetectable via thought anchors (J8LcYQLXpY); and MIRAGE, which immunises images by exploiting commercial editors' pre-generation moderation (wdZxGtKvh6).
