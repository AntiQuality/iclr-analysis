# b20 qualitative notes (173 papers)

- **Agent memory is a new attack surface in 2027.** Persistent and shared memory shows up both as a target and as a way failures spread. Examples: sleeper memory poisoning that stays dormant and comes back in later sessions, reported on GPT-5.6 Sol and Kimi-K2.6 (0imYw5ajm4); invalid procedures passed to successor agents through shared notes (OGqiokpRCr); stale or superseded constraints in consolidated memory (h7doXt0W8x); false-memory evaluation (ECXsauXWek); answer-preservation audits for memory-augmented LLMs (9SrtI40SR5). There is nothing comparable among the 2026 papers in this batch.
- **AI control and monitoring are maturing, especially for agents.** 2027 adds covert malicious fine-tuning by internally deployed agents (Claude Opus 4.7 on an extended PostTrainBench) (De1C9JO09d), and a game-theoretic case for reviewing a window of steps instead of single steps against decomposition attacks (DecomposedHarm) (7iTakBZs8W). Others cover deterrence-based oversight of colluding multi-provider reviewers (DOmb6CZd1q) and audits showing that better offline monitor predictors can make worse runtime alarms (68xaUEyTkf). In 2026 the oversight work centred on CoT: RL-induced motivated reasoning (ZMrdV4Ysia) and reasoning-trace auditing (8jlQCOQp5J).
- **Agent threat models are moving from injected text to authorization and supply chain.** Examples: whether agents disclose data on self-asserted identity (SaysWho, kXZkkYseBp); contextual-integrity checks before an agent commits an action (XYpOY14bC4); functional counterfeit tools that return correct output but have hidden side effects (40R626leak); malicious agent-skill detection (yB3veAHcW4); cross-channel composition attacks on web agents with C3 Bench (uLbaNB7y4W); multi-agent denial-of-service through recursive calls (rjTyRLJ2R1). Defenses are increasingly pre-execution gateways or commit protocols rather than prompt filters (MedGauge yyPDGf8fN9, GoalSeal wvkbSz7VnD).
- **Deception and sandbagging work is turning into evaluation methodology.** Examples: a framework for checking what a model can hide during capability tests (aIF8NerUEh); multi-direction deception probes with context-dependent gating, framed as a fix for single-direction probes (5eIQxFegpM); subliminal preference transfer through near-duplicate image curation in VLMs, including Gemma 4 and Qwen3-VL (0Wnc6JBDM0). The 2026 precursor is strategic dishonesty fooling output-based jailbreak monitors (IbDr8xgUMW).
- **Inference-cost and resource-exhaustion attacks are an "OTHER" niche worth tracking.** Examples: a bit-flip (Rowhammer) attack that suppresses EOS for all users (DbHtCaO4RD, 2026); adversarial inputs that cause excessive reasoning in reasoning models (g0uBExrnjz, 2026); multi-agent denial-of-service (rjTyRLJ2R1, 2027).
- **Safety training ideas are moving from post-training to pretraining.** Examples: reusing native safety from safety-pretrained models through on-policy distillation (vAV0RGwA0A); a separate control channel learned during pretraining, "steerable by design" (Mancelle-3B, hW8MtlRzY7). The jailbreak work otherwise follows familiar lines: audio red-teaming with ARENA against Audio Flamingo 3, Qwen2-Audio and GPT-Audio (T3KGhnOUL6); soft-prompt adversarial training (CqxBL8BhfC); attacker-defender co-evolution (X7CESP8IIw); over-refusal boundary learning, where SafetyFlip reports HarmBench and XSTest results (7J2DXTCEwz).
- **Recurring and stable themes.** Deepfake and forgery work in 2027 now includes attacks on the detectors themselves (as65TxCJSO, PpOI7Udsps). Watermarking is spreading to new modalities: DNA (vo1qxcMxxx), speech TTS (FIpctuE7GT) and diffusion latents (y3rnzfgUk0). Privacy work stays focused on DP federated LoRA (gDkZzcCvtl, BPzSV4uw0x), DP auditing (MRdrxZsxyG) and DP synthetic-text benchmarks (ContinuousBench, cTkFkFZ8GF). More 2027 papers in the safety area are statistically framed audits with certificates (1roHwQ9Wdi, 9SrtI40SR5, frKRE58U02), many of them hedged and full of caveats.
- **Labelling caveats.** Several 2027 "safety-area" submissions are only loosely safety-related: LLM human-behaviour simulators (w5JVJiFnuQ), energy use of latent multi-agent communication (UX27QPxdhq) and proactive desktop agents (htWKZVku7f). I gave these rel=0, so raw counts by area will overstate safety volume.
