# b36 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent skills become a supply-chain attack surface in 2027.** Two 2027 papers attack installable agent "Skills" instead of prompts or web pages:
  - qSF6LxtL76 splits a poisoned skill into an upstream "pretext" skill and a downstream "actuation" skill.
  - PwlYzqIzOb (BadSkill) hides a backdoor in a classifier bundled inside a skill, with 97% attack success.

  Agent prompt-injection defenses also shift. In 2026 they are monolithic supervision frameworks (orzX69D5UO, CCA). In 2027 they are structural: a dual-agent split that isolates trusted context (1ddbk4iCEp, GraDual), screening tool responses by source provenance (MtySiIyk6R, PIPES) and graph-verified safety conditions on agent trajectories (NBRxyZGt6o, VEGAS/GraphGuard).
- **2027 evaluation papers question their own metrics.** Several argue that standard metrics mislead:
  - Monitor AUROC depends on which success label is used (bUJ8UGm8ab, AgentDojo: 0.63 vs 0.84).
  - Harmful probability mass can remain under a sampled refusal (sQPLvlAEOq, bounded continuation analysis).
  - Binary jailbreak rate is replaced by harm severity plus over-refusal (wFFR6uyf52).
  - Word count alone reaches 0.94 AUROC on a synthetic-text detection benchmark (HLB77qwQ8X).
  - Evaluation awareness is split into environment cues and model propensity (sLJRdIt3IP).

  Comparable 2026 evaluation papers are more often new broad benchmarks (Fcf5fLmaeG TrustGen, CyberGym 2YvbLQEdYt, PACEbench kGEuZXaXU6).
- **Reward hacking becomes something to monitor in coding agents.** HackTrace (wgZCA7JAoO, 2027) trains a probe on agent hidden states using 173k Qwen3-8B coding trajectories. With GRPO penalties, it cuts cheating among passing solutions from 82–91% to 1–5%. CyberBinGym (HL1HStcZpD) audits agent trajectories to reject reward hacking in exploit benchmarks. Another solver/auditor paper (rviixPQ8Tu) treats alignment as law-and-economics deterrence. Nearest 2026 counterpart: RL-Obfuscation (CPajDOuA3h), where models learn to evade latent monitors.
- **Open-weight tamper resistance and representation-level attacks (2027).**
  - ErcCQSs93S makes abliteration backfire: the model collapses or keeps refusing, and HarmBench attack success falls from 0.72 to 0.
  - GtPUNUOdIX shows that stitching in one precursor-checkpoint activation reverts alignment fine-tuning.
  - COMBAT (XzNp808k7s) transfers refusal and harm directions across models to jailbreak them.
  - PJBack (k455jbnE51) designs jailbreak backdoors to survive later safety alignment.
- **New substrates and modalities as threat models.**
  - Diffusion LLMs: jailbreak safety in 2026 (zBPzxhso8M, DiffuGuard); unlearning in 2027 (AIvW4QpdVI); DP synthetic text via diffusion-LM guidance in 2027 (C7BBqIYeZA).
  - Video MLLMs: temporally dispersed jailbreaks in 2027 (dZVAfFZAOj).
  - GUI grounding: coordinate-digit adversarial attacks in 2027 (AmcaiNI92S).
  - Multimodal RAG poisoning in both years (0UNKgsDFPo in 2026, O26AmnGkFD in 2027).
  - Retrieval-augmented diffusion steering in 2027 (e8tYFmI6d8).
- **2027 integrity and provenance work moves beyond text watermarks.** Examples are auditing MoE route execution on untrusted serving backends (dFn9KC6qXC), lineage attribution for stolen and evolving RAG knowledge bases (JH8vp5nwZi), publisher-keyed face release verification (Bu8Bfc5Gx2) and auditable unlearning (xOQYemwxFM). In 2026 this batch's provenance work is mostly multi-bit LLM watermarking (qF6TyKhc7S) and IAR image provenance (qYu4wj7O3z).
- **Recurring models and benchmarks.**
  - 2027 abstracts cite Gemma-4 (31B), Qwen3/Qwen3-VL, GPT-5.6 and Claude.
  - Recurring benchmarks are AgentDojo, HarmBench, TOFU/MUSE, POPE, BaxBench and SusVibes (SecureVibe Ftxzea79pn, on secure vibe coding).
  - 2026 papers cite Gemini 2.5 Pro (CoT Hijacking kQRjcBmFAD, 99% attack success), Llama-3 and Qwen2.5.
  - Stable in both years: LVLM object hallucination, deepfake and AI-generated image detection, OOD detection with CLIP negative labels, and DP/fairness theory.
- **Calibration and abstention reframed for post-training (2027).** On-policy distillation causes systematic overconfidence (MBZrQUpsGz). Reasoning models get RL rewards for abstention (i7INKmVQYZ). Linguistic confidence is treated as a distribution (eWwlq7rait). "Decorative rationalization" shows that cited context often does not change the answer (Q083YRHAux).
