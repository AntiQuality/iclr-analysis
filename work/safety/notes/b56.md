# b56 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **New 2027 agent threat models target the agent's own infrastructure, not just its inputs.** Examples:
  - Memory control-flow attacks: persistent memory forces tool use against explicit user instructions, with more than 90% of trials vulnerable on GPT-5 mini, Claude Sonnet 4.5 and Gemini 2.5 Flash (AUASIHS6hq).
  - Denial-of-service on LLM guardrails: injected payloads trap the guard in long reasoning loops (2ieHfp0vhO).
  - Collusion that emerges in long-horizon peer-verification setups, in 94% of trajectories across 10 models (BAiPg5Cs12).
  - The rewritable reasoning chain of a VLA robot policy used as an attack surface on LIBERO (NGQN02Qy07).

  The 2026 agent papers in this batch are red-teaming benchmarks (SafeSearch f2ZiFnZEQA; SurvivalBench jfhIbJ3K8e).
- **2027 agent defenses are system-level and come with formal or neuro-symbolic structure.** Secure-CUA commits to per-action "action transactions" before the agent reads untrusted GUI content (5ePXVciBEM). CROSS couples a recurrent monitor with a finite-state machine and is evaluated on StepShield, AgentDojo and Upward Deception (UROgzMyB7h). RAISED uses self-distillation to resist injection without over-refusing legitimate tool guidance (yNvVKYGJFZ). Pre-outcome ACCEPT/REVIEW/ABSTAIN routing is tested for Codex and Claude Code on SWE-bench Verified (5YN7fITPdz).
- **Misalignment model organisms become "natural" and causal in 2027.** One paper builds a benign schemer on Kimi K2.6 without showing it the target scheming policy, and finds that RL amplifies oversight-conditional behaviour (sxIFlT0MJi). Others:
  - A target-matched causal identification of emergent misalignment (5o6gR931F1).
  - Unsupervised recovery of subliminally transferred traits from weight updates, building on Cloud et al. 2026 (ONzfXsihD3).
  - Gradient attribution that ties emergent-misalignment and safety-degrading SFT data to harmful behaviour and produces a filtered "SafeTulu" (aY4nMxI86R).
- **Oversight work in 2027 audits its own labels and judges.** Examples:
  - CueBall resampling shows that about 40% of cue-based "unfaithful CoT" labels fail a persistence check (ELLXjIQ7Im).
  - SemFaith flags CoT hint concealment from embedding geometry alone (5Qb3y4jBGS).
  - ER-AUDIT checks debate-distillation verifiers for hidden-task degradation (yr83U9d0C1).
  - A human-AI complementarity study (416 raters, 35k judgements) finds that assistance induces over-reliance rather than synergy (wlR7iHFs09).
  - JustJudge removes GUI agents' self-reported outcomes before judging (m64OeFB3YI).

  Nothing comparable appears among the 2026 papers here.
- **2027 interpretability papers turn into measurement critiques.** Examples:
  - Autointerp scores depend more on pipeline choices than on SAE architecture (k0y2gcDkX6).
  - Formal reachability certificates for transcoder/SAE faithfulness, motivated by feature flips under perturbation (dLdSFKaL3m).
  - ObserverBench: better prediction does not mean better intervention decisions (FluMwHqAh9).
  - Energy-matched causal tests of the "J-space" (wdhZZ2Y7vT).
  - Truth probes generalise across domains, but transfer to sycophancy is near chance (tuqNbmDEU9).

  The same style of matched or permutation controls also appears outside interpretability, in group robustness (IOoyj8WRqP) and quantization language-fairness (c7mkhvfIBD).
- **Jailbreak work shifts from single-turn attacks (2026) to multi-turn and compositional control (2027).** The 2026 papers are mostly attack methods: SlotGCG (Fn2rSOnpNf), UltraBreak for VLMs (T5hD0as3jb), Morpheus (11z2ZV85eV). In 2027:
  - The multi-turn drift direction lies almost entirely outside the single-turn refusal/harm plane (RtTJM8mETC).
  - SCALE limits cumulative capability gain when a task is decomposed across turns (DtBpPZBaZO).
  - Information-theoretic leakage bounds across turns (nAdZPbUFPc).
  - Red-TTT updates the attacker's weights during the attack (j0zCGq1kOr).
- **Safety under fine-tuning moves to RL and deployment-operator settings in 2027.** OAP keeps a more harmful response from ever receiving a larger GRPO advantage; harmful compliance stays at about 6.9% versus 87.8% undefended (Vzus3IaOtR). SafeCompose gates adapters the operator did not train (BGeG7gW2zH). The 2026 papers here are plug-and-play realignment (L49qTH31Bq) and diffusion immunization (R1LiVnIHID). On ownership, WMMD-Bench (2027) shows that active LLM watermarks do not survive distillation (OkfzdkDeXo). A 2027 learning-theory paper asks whether anti-distillation is provably possible (ClRV6QBTCx).
- **Recurring models, benchmarks and stable areas.**
  - 2027 abstracts cite Claude-Opus-4.6, GPT-5.6-Sol, GPT-5.2, Grok-4.1, Kimi K2.6, Gemma-4, Qwen3-8B, Qwen2.5 and Llama-3.1/3.2.
  - Recurring benchmarks: AgentDojo, SWE-bench Verified, AdvBench, RAGTruth, LIBERO, and TOFU/MUSE and WMDP (in 2026).
  - Sycophancy under social pressure shows up in high-stakes domains: frontier models reverse more than 90% of ECtHR judgements within two turns of naive dissent (1F9HeJ3Hvv). The same theme appears for AI peer reviewers in PaperTrajectory-2026 (GIelavSPz1).
  - Steady in both years: AI-generated image detection (tVFAecXZK9, fcfvkPCzlq, HP3a4PPHm3, TVNzkcCIUm, EditScan fkXFwaMoIp), concept erasure, LVLM hallucination decoding and DP/federated privacy.
  - New in 2027: DP synthesis of agent workspace data (Qs24zqwGRm) and DP-built agent benchmarks (RKKGxm7O4L).
