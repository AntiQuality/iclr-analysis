# b15 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent skills/plug-ins as a new attack surface (2027):** the "skill layer" now shows up as a security boundary. FORTIS (i7twcxEXJ6) benchmarks over-privileged skill selection. CRIME (PoA3faBQqn) composes skills that each pass vetting into malicious behaviour. SCEPTER (OBa3owju9Z) and SIFG (UTrtEyCnqs) argue that provenance or certificate-only enforcement cannot tell an attack from a benign run when the agent regenerates untrusted content. Neither threat model appears among the 2026 papers in this batch.
- **Environment-side and social manipulation of agents (2027):** CAVEAT (kTuIMbhP1E) has marketplaces with their own incentives steering computer-use agents (user-optimal purchases fall from 78.6% to 17.3%). Vigilance (JHzqvlgSH1) covers malicious advice, scams and unsafe PRs. CrossSurf (n98bmSoIl0) does cross-surface prompt injection on mobile GUI agents. "Work as payment" (d5E2lwjO32) finds that web agents ignore cost. In 2026 the agent-safety papers were mostly misuse or harm benchmarks (CUAHarm Esntu2V82P, SafeMind 8bjuA06JXB).
- **Multi-agent safety becomes adversarial and structural (2027):** the work covers coalition and collusion detection in debate (LATTICE mWzUTxY6q2), adversarial capture predicted from graph topology (Pc8P3kCecq), reputation-based robust aggregation (MiniRep EvAw2OPG25) and activation-based correction of compromised agents (CompassGuard aWeTP9FH13). The 2026 equivalent was descriptive, e.g. agents "echoing" each other (CkZAO6tDHv). Agent-native social platforms (Moltbook stereotypes, Eut4tG3ro9) appear as a new study site.
- **Interpretability turns to auditing and guards (2027):** SAE audits get limits of detection, using canaries and decoys (nYJ5p5voZ4). Refusal policies are recovered from behaviour as executable constitutions (AyOaCG4B4G). Harmful agent trajectories are read from guard-model internal states (XFHXlREKbQ). Steering is used to adapt guardrail judges (YUBbbDwjcp) and to remove ownership bias in verifiers (OwbT4EloeN). In 2026 the interpretability-for-safety papers were more about refusal directions themselves (ROSI 8c2SbG5PLj, linear decoding of refused knowledge DSIZapaKKE).
- **Evaluation-validity critiques are recurring in 2027:** unlearning methods that retrain on the retain set can pass evaluation while damaging untested facts (M6F3f0A89g). PRM step boundaries change which candidate wins (yJZolyPCmU). A frozen CLIP "safety score" in safe RL tracks its caption bank rather than the hazard (l9i64YY39j). Agent-harness monitoring does not show self-improvement (pRp5t6yrJ2). The style is pre-registered and uses matched controls with negative controls.
- **Reward hacking moves to rubric and non-verifiable RL:** in 2026, "Miracle Steps" (FGkknrhv09) dealt with outcome-reward hacking in math. In 2027 the topics are rubric-recall hacking (FORK sMy13QhmQd) and reward-model style bias in agent group RL (ARC mAVRIcwe25). Deception is evaluated in agentic, stateful settings under pressure (AgentDecept TmHLZgUPa3) and by the gap between confidence and belief (Mz1BjZ8hK7). Both extend 2026's text-only Mesa&Mask benchmark (0lW2UBiEWN).
- **Steady areas:** both years have many papers on AI-generated image detection and deepfakes (generalisation to unseen generators: PhysCons, GSD, CRAFT), concept erasure versus restoration attacks in diffusion (VisualErase, CASE, RECORD), harmful fine-tuning defences (SafeSchatten, CCTR) and federated backdoors and privacy. Newer 2027 angles are anti-distillation as IP protection (DistilShield oAMmUnkAAA), PII extraction from video by VLMs (Video-PIE 8U7sg8RgeT) and active-deception jailbreak defence through decoy replies (META-TRAP jMFbdAu8wH). Models and benchmarks that recur: Qwen2.5/Qwen-VL, Llama, LLaVA, HarmBench, TOFU/MUSE, GenImage, Terminal-Bench 2.0.
