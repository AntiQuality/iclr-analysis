# b39 qualitative notes (173 papers; 2026 vs 2027)

- **Persistent-state agent attacks are new in 2027.** In 2026 the agent-security papers here are about defending against prompt injection with information-flow control, with autonomy metrics on AgentDojo/WASP (g0aVCDY3gS). The 2027 attacks target state that persists across sessions. MemGhost (u94T9UWCzY) plants poisoned memory through one email and is tested on WhisperBench over real IMAP/SMTP. ClawTrojan (hRoHqaGh9b) uses multi-step trojans in local agent harnesses. CORSA (x7fntC2OV8) optimises skill injections so they get past skill routers. MCEF (28qvccNuTQ) spreads fabricated, instruction-free evidence across several sources. "Skills", "harness" and "memory" are the new attack-surface words.
- **Agent privacy moves from outputs to the whole trajectory.** Σ-Bench/PrivacyTrace (T1zcJOQFMQ) finds data-flow violations in 83–99% of agent runs, against 33–55% leakage in final outputs. PRISM (5lk0NlZc4f) runs Bayesian-surrogate red-teaming of multi-agent privacy leaks. PRIVACT (B0HCvb3GJ7) measures sensitive conclusions inferred over many turns. Contextual-integrity work (rT7fnADPPs, on PrivacyLens+ and ConfAIde+) is a recurring 2027 thread.
- **Monitorability and steganography matured.** In 2026, q4qxtaKVAU found only early signs of steganography in frontier models. In 2027, tBCMCACAfN trains an MEC-based steganography model organism at 0.57 bits per token: a text-feature classifier detects it near chance (AUC 0.62), while a full-logit classifier reaches AUC 1.0, and paraphrasing with Claude Haiku 4.5 disrupts the channel. CoT-faithfulness work also moves from latent-variable inversion (bpmQnmnvp1, 2026) to causal tests using SAE concept ablation (WZBavPTogb, 2027). Subliminal learning under soft and on-policy distillation (Tj8ZsGcoAv) is a new 2027 risk of hidden traits spreading.
- **Many 2027 papers critique how things are measured.** Examples:
  - Jailbreak ASR is inflated by agreement between the attack's supervisor and the evaluator, plus semantic drift (v4Fb6JeHUe).
  - Hallucination metrics need a placebo baseline so a model cannot score better just by saying less (1yC9ZMBWCz, SCAR).
  - Video-hallucination gains need motion-blind control arms (djIRyJcNKn).
  - Best-of-N scores for multimodal PRMs are flawed (PfhSe4a0GY).
  - Refusal directions depend on how they are identified (O2kpAvVFtB).
  - SAE features depend on seeds and duplicated data (EjInprGpk9 in 2026, wV3PB7CFsy in 2027).

  Typical 2026 papers in the batch propose a new method and report state-of-the-art numbers.
- **Reward hacking and sycophancy show up in new places.** Co-cheating between proposer and solver in self-evolving search agents (Id4G8bzzKO). Rhetorical rewriting that games AI peer reviewers, built on ICLR 2026 manuscripts (oBb0uicpGo). Whether sycophantic judgment changes persist on medical and legal tasks with no reference answer (BUZoqCgWXn, reporting a Claude Opus 5 rate of 72.7% on PrinciplismQA).
- **The models under test are new.** 2027 abstracts name GPT-6, GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Opus 5, Qwen3/3.5 and Olmo-3.1. Diffusion LLMs are now targets in their own right: dLLM watermarking (eJlHaKpRWZ), membership inference on LLaDA and Dream (DedRNvfkYc), and dLLMs used to generate data for adversarial training (2dNbwYy8Ho). New deployment-time threat models include quantization-conditioned backdoors via attention sinks (BXUnpS9Y2b), safety erosion during test-time training (62qsX8Btgx), and an LLM judging physical risks putting protection of a person ahead of larger physical consequences ("Protection Mode", I35nG4HR52).
- **Steady areas.** Watermarking and AI-generated-content detection stay large in both years:
  - 2026: capacity (Ry8jLSYIUG), unbiased-watermark benchmark UWBench (6T4LR1oRwA).
  - 2027: MoE-router watermarking (WzAAxUKLPF), serving co-design (pjOeMOuTxa), fingerprinting via watermark distillation (ZExlJYlSn6), audio-visual deepfake localization (yh1cPrsdi4).

  MLLM/VLM hallucination mitigation (TAer6K7scZ, OOXMVZUKNr, NrJ9XaVHDD, W1GdEjiyzl) and SAE-centred interpretability are also steady. Harmful fine-tuning work in 2026 includes a unified tamper benchmark, TamperBench (fXn4Rk8B3l), and elicitation attacks that fine-tune open models on safeguarded frontier outputs (viBAbg9ihM).
