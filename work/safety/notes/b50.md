# b50 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **Monitors and lie detectors are now attacked and stress-tested, not only built (2027).** TMRvp5RVjQ shows models learn to evade latent activation monitors from verdict feedback alone; a rank-1 LoRA cuts the monitor's TPR from 100% to 4%. XQGNmgRz8U finds that lie-detection probes follow role-play persona beliefs and spurious correlates. N50ZtvHdp4 predicts agent deception from hidden states several calls before it happens. 7qA0ewB7oK ("blind fibers") formalises verifier blind spots that arise when the trajectory representation drops safety-relevant information. Yk09iKovaA trains for CoT–internal-computation agreement. The 2026 oversight papers here are formal and game-theoretic instead (QWopGahUEL AI-Control games, KR8viVTrX4 HITL legal formalisation).
- **New 2027 agent and infrastructure threat models:**
  - A malicious agent that gets itself selected by a dynamic orchestrator and then bypasses guardrails (sQKQkJf3cv TrojanAgent).
  - Corpus poisoning that traps agentic RAG in retrieval loops to exhaust resources (QPYkMnE5mx CyclicRA).
  - Adversarial images that inflate adaptive VLM token budgets (DTo0wuEcRm).
  - Tool-call policies that frameworks accept but never enforce, audited across 18 frameworks (PHwBZoutef).
  - Watermarks in unified multimodal models that leak earlier chat content (DROnjDHq58).
  - Graph-based detection of distributed memory-poisoning attacks (8VgRLJU5Gf GRAD).

  The 2026 counterparts are single-channel: system-prompt poisoning (rlsVuhVFpv) and distractor injection into reasoning models (bIJkCBVSgd).
- **Diffusion language models become a safety target in 2027.** Three papers apply standard safety methods to DLMs: unlearning (T5I5uQQEbi LRCU, evaluated on WMDP/RWKU/TOFU with LLaDA), hallucination detection through "early factual commitment" (l8dP3SS3RI), and safety-guided denoising (O7gwtEz2lE SADDER). None of the 2026 papers in this batch target DLMs.
- **More rigour and more certificates in 2027.** Several papers lead with statistical caution:
  - Seed-level instability of SafeDPO refusal rates, which range from 1.8% to 98.5% across seeds (yi7Z4AHGsE).
  - Item-level intervals that overstate parity between fine-tuning recipes (pgZflfdvUd).
  - Aggregation order changing which circuits activation patching finds (IptgDngQeh).
  - Complexity results for certifying non-leakage (mVJzfFoRun).
  - Gauge-symmetry limits on LLM verification (mtJUSjOIv2).
  - Coverage-guaranteed model provenance sets (Cg9hNQjlg1).
  - Cryptographic verifiable inference for Mamba-3 (288sdvBc8j) and private dense retrieval (1e9jAnBoYb).
- **Safety training becomes adaptive and context-aware.** Red-teaming is folded into training loops: BoundaryPlay counterfactual self-play (T2JoJkvPgP) and RedLoop persistent strategy libraries (htdcolcmT2). Guardrails become policy-conditioned: DynaGuard in 2026 (gc8Ylt0lbm), then in 2027 a constitutional-AI approach across modalities (BkBXNhY68c AnyCAI) and long-context safety reward models (2GPDxcF2sh ContextFlip). Safety preservation under deployment transforms continues: SafeQ quantization (70RbLmZVvW) and MoE expert compression with fairness and safety costs (DR0qHuYqlL).
- **Sycophancy and social harms are framed through interpretability and companionship.** sRpurB4TaG uses SAE feature injection on Qwen3.5 (including 35B-A3B) and finds that reducing sycophancy does not improve refusal. EUDAIMONIA (FjFgIPVp2X) benchmarks harmful intimacy and dependence across 26 LLMs, naming Grok-4.7, Claude-Opus-5.5 and GPT-6-Astra. i6lgdgxIre audits advice that silently depends on user values the user never stated. vvqYd6B0M6 steers goals by transplanting a "value axis" and reduces test-gaming in Qwen3-8B and GPT-OSS-20B.
- **Recurring models, benchmarks and steady topics.** The 2027 papers cite Qwen3/Qwen3.5, Qwen3-VL, GPT-OSS-20B, GLM-5.3, GPT-5.4 (as a judge in GyWDjjkfVb), MedGemma and Mamba-3. The 2026 papers cite GPT-4o, GPT-4.1, Llama-3 and LLaVA-1.5. Common benchmarks are TOFU, WMDP, RWKU, JailbreakBench, HarmBench, AgentDojo, HaluEval, TruthfulQA and BBQ. Both years have a steady volume of AI-generated image detection (N1PhdtojjK, FMXamHI1EF, vRqTYnW9ss), LLM text watermarking (byte-level ByteMark losZJYZ6xK, multi-bit TrellisMark vIS18zNPOe) and LLM/diffusion unlearning. The 2027 work is about generalisation, for example detectors that avoid dataset shortcuts and watermarks that are independent of the tokenizer.
