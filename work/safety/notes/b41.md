# b41 observations (173 papers; 2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent supply-chain and environment threats are new in 2027.** Malicious "Agent Skills" hidden by code obfuscation are run in real harnesses: Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI and OpenCode (uAeJBSQOH8). Other 2027 threat models: dark-pattern web UIs that steer agents into unauthorized consequences (ao5d1VpleM, TrickyArena/WebDecept), behavioural side-channel leakage of user secrets by browser agents (YQeWgbsnPt, AgentTell), longitudinal memory-induced violations in OpenClaw workspaces (j1LLFYK4lF), and misleading collaborator feedback for coding agents (R7ub0QvlFK, TrapArena). The 2026 agent papers here are mostly injection or red-teaming benchmarks: TAMAS (80oiymEbaO) and SWExploit (PVarNsrX2v).
- **Benchmarks of offensive cyber capability by agents are a 2027 cluster.** MobileCybench (aDoHo9F2BW) evaluates GPT-5.5, GPT-5.6, GLM-5.2, Opus 4.8 and Opus 5 on Android vulnerability discovery. ProtoArena (kksz86yafY) covers cross-component protocol exploits. PlanSmuggle (wFnBBjkwDr) attacks Qwen3, DeepSeek-V4-Pro and GPT-5 with compositional unsafe plans that pass every per-action check. None of these appear in the 2026 papers of this batch.
- **Benign fine-tuning that erodes refusals is a recurring 2027 storyline, now with mechanistic and RL variants.** Examples: 1,000 benign samples unlearn refusal (DStFCYLdVC), an "opening token" loss-term defense tested on 17 models (HdUlQV6dde), a multi-stage RL unalignment attack (mVLyBC4sRI), transferable safety task vectors (2fAXwfIawS), and autoregressive-consistency theory of shallow alignment (l09t6heq83). In 2026 the line was represented by benign-data attacks such as TrojanPraise (ZcxSBLmQm4).
- **Interpretability is shifting toward methodological scepticism and model organisms.** 2027 papers include lexical and untrained-network baselines that undercut evaluation-awareness probes (WXjd30H2gJ, EvalAwareBench, 17 models, pre-registered), wrongness controls for deception-probe transfer (8dVFPMlbDO), and a recipe for validating model organisms for alignment auditing (ELbKq3PrHc). The 2026 papers are more constructive steering and monitoring tools, such as persona vectors (20DsUSauCj) and COS-Steering (vzXyVNCGAL).
- **Diffusion language models are a new target surface in 2027.** One paper watermarks masked-diffusion LMs (Dv97ivGjui, TANGO, which also covers frequency-based forgery). Another gives a mechanistic account of "benign-shifted compliance" under prefix jailbreaks (Uu1fbKxEAb). Speculative decoding also gets an availability attack (BJACmAEfW6).
- **The style of the 2027 papers is more statistical and audit-focused.** Many have pre-registered or controlled audits, confidence intervals and Holm corrections. Examples: memorization audits of code models needing non-member controls (lV3nwd3dll), spacing effects on memorisation (32bXbTKcXx), membership-inference-based audits of certified unlearning (qNaZE6FrhA), FDR-controlled behaviour discovery (NAn4qt5nJJ), and a judgment-execution gap / false-completion benchmark (llvFlbPzRF, NoGoBench). Exact unlearning theory, often in bandit settings, is a niche cluster (cEmuTAg4qE, 1VvStbHUzg, vo4Diesi8h).
- **Provenance work stays steady across both years but broadens in 2027.** In 2027 it covers cross-modal text and image binding watermarks (CLXuMd70AP), cryptographic watermarks for tabular data against dataset fabrication (hPoiz4zaEh), and dataset watermarks for detecting what was trained on (a8miLdXlN9), alongside many detectors of AI-generated images. Deepfake detection in 2026 centred on benchmarks and red-teaming: MAVOS-DD (uUYcKsPNgM) and FoeGlass (gwi2T2CsfK).
