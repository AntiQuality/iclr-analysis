# b17 qualitative notes (173 papers, 2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent attack surface moves toward the supply chain and to agent-to-agent channels (2027).** New threat models include semantic supply-chain attacks on agent skill registries via SKILL.md metadata (Nk1Pwr4veT), cross-agent "dormant agent" triggering via emotion-steered images in VLM multi-agent systems (feSoknGite), composition attacks over memory, messages and tools with sharp theoretical bounds (4rjZXN9rtI), permission-flow and confused-deputy verification for multi-agent systems (pZvv6oTrju), and wrapper format (XML vs JSON) as a driver of injection success (ydKWnK5tjp). In 2026 the agent work here is mostly guardrail datasets (WebGuard, BQQ34bv25t) and generic multi-agent red-teaming (yjG3qagrZ0).
- **Defenses with guarantees, and audits of the defenses themselves (2027).** ATTRACK (0QXPPsbddF) is a certified taint-tracking defense against indirect prompt injection evaluated on AgentDojo. GuardValue (iHWmn52wwn) measures the causal value of pre-execution guards on tau2-bench. PU learning is used to audit monitor false alarms (e5TY7nqvjv). Outcome-aware MAS auditing appears in MOSAIC (IUKnax4deH). Overall, 2027 shifts from building guardrails to measuring whether monitors and guards actually help.
- **Deception, evaluation awareness and self-report reliability become measurable topics (2027).** Examples are format cues that make prompts look like tests (Z9PwmPQpT7), deception probes failing under steered lies in a Werewolf game (OGpuwplImJ), control decisions such as refusal computed outside the verbalizable workspace, which matters for CoT monitoring (jKkmhmNU7y), self-report vs behavior dissociation on risk-taking (sKcibXcqfE), and two kinds of sycophancy, wish-following vs belief-following (29n7h2xUuq). The batch's 2026 papers have no comparable work.
- **Reward hacking is reframed as evaluator gaming in agentic and self-improving loops (2027).** Examples are scaffold self-improvement raising visible scores while held-out targets fall (C6EElQ2PRG), "evidence collapse" in verifiable-reward agents that choose what to observe (xjYqszWAhz), dense progress rewards misleading RL red-team attackers (Q7cULvkx0X), and Lookahead decoding to predict RLHF failures from the RM before training (6a4v7CpeAu). The 2026 counterpart is classic RM uncertainty (PURM, KfsK8ybjaP).
- **Interpretability for safety goes to finer grain and moves into training (2027).** Examples are head-level provenance of harm detection across base, instruct and abliterated checkpoints (2eoMtzq03h), the attention-recognizes / MLP-writes refusal pathway across 18 Base-Instruct pairs (PqQcRNSxQy), when safety concepts become readable during pretraining (u561w8hVNT), persona-drift forecasting from hidden states (EbihLCFzfD), and steering trust toward users (0TuoFvyMQ1). 2026 work here is broader evaluation, for example SteeringSafety side effects (VsEkWh3mXu).
- **New model families and output channels get safety studies.** Examples are diffusion LLMs (inference-time steering and remasking defense, HMD0xk4A6J), grounding outputs such as points and boxes that bypass VLM refusals by 31-59 pp (zQZVpPwjbo), compositional "distributed implicit harm" in video moderation (ulmVvB9rHq), phone-use agent misuse on real social-media apps (mTLAJN9VpV), and composable attacks triggered by quantization, pruning or merging (DdeRtXKXHr). Frontier models named in 2027 abstracts include GPT-5, Claude Sonnet 5, Qwen3/Qwen3-VL, Gemma-3/4, DeepSeek V4 and GLM-5.3.
- **Stable, recurring threads across both years.** These are LVLM object-hallucination decoding fixes (7QVxGhSPYy, wJYVjubJp3), deepfake and machine-generated-text detection (dGS68egMIP, WMS0ypYjwm, 6IuZfDGv6D, wjO8F2KPxN), concept erasure and its reversal (kJkV7iqjLq, m4BUqnjx1P), transfer attacks on closed-source MLLMs (ERU9cDYyta, jUi9UTLeNp), and LLM unlearning that can be recovered or goes only shallow (7bZuhygFB1, rkC5vGGki3 multi-hop "deep unlearning"). IP and provenance work grows in 2027 with distillation attribution (wbOqOEgNeY) and RAG dataset watermarking (NXdKUfQ8fN).
- **Statistical rigor and theory in safety evaluation increase (2027).** Examples are pre-registered decision rules, validated discovered differences in model-replacement audits (v1r8P28MYV), framing effects of red-teaming instructions (6uPlbJvwcb), defender-centric Shapley valuation of jailbreaks (ZrZReH7xV1), solvable scaling laws for pre-training poisoning (2XH9Pc5030), and compute-gap theory for transferable attacks (FIakJL1eiv).
