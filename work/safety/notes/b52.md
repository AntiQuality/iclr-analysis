# b52 qualitative notes (2026 vs 2027)

- **New 2027 attack surfaces in the agent supply chain and agent infrastructure.** These go beyond classic injection:
  - Bytecode-cache substitution in third-party agent skills that evades skill scanners (INhzfdaeHY, PyCache Trap).
  - Steganographic injection that exploits the runtime information gap between a content auditor and the executing agent (Qw913dVTOM, AsymStego on OpenClaw with DeepSeek-V4-Flash, GPT-5.5 as auditor).
  - Poisoning the trajectories used by self-evolving safety harnesses (xSucIKMpyw, TrajPoison).
  - Revoked memory surviving in derived plans and summaries (e1TGZCKFiU, RevBound).
  - Bypassing shell denylists in terminal agents (KyjyVpKZU5).
  - Compositional cross-service harms in personal agents (CmA5r5AjJT) and harmful-task decomposition into benign subtasks (tnP3MMqSfb, DeCompBench).

  In this batch, the 2026 agent work is mostly broad benchmarking (xggSxCFQbA, OpenAgentSafety).
- **2027 agent defenses move to system-level harnesses with formal guarantees.** CORA uses conformal risk control to bound harmful execution by GUI agents (QAaESzjDeK, Phone-Harm). AEGIS is a neuro-symbolic sidecar verifier with a LLaDA-8B scorer, benchmarked against GPT-5.5 and Opus-4.8 judges (ja1rRqY7Yj). EvoSafeHarness searches for model- and domain-specific harnesses (bhdqCizNqu). WardenGuard routes through trust-annotated channels to a judge cascade (LgrSflvzMW). One defense renders untrusted text as images, exploiting a modality gap in instruction following (JR458V7Irc). Another gives information-theoretic certificates for prompt-injection detection and instruction-hierarchy violations (6banCM00vU).
- **Multi-agent and population-scale safety grows in 2027.** AgentWorld runs about 50B tokens over long-horizon agent worlds and finds that agents recognize threats yet still act on them (oj8njANLtc). Other papers study the spread of false beliefs, framed as the Mandela effect, in agent social networks (iB5EwVlfF1), Byzantine-resilient quorum consensus (dFobSoJ39d), and multi-agent sycophancy localized to pretrained mid-layers rather than RLHF (MvYGMIlpWB). The 2026 precursor is Moloch's Bargain, where competitive audience optimization drives disinformation (r0CX4jgWTe).
- **Oversight and reward hacking turn adversarial and theoretical in 2027.** Examples:
  - Provable canonical paraphrasing against LM steganography (dHKm7EbLhx).
  - Monitors co-trained with workers to prevent monitor evasion, with a Littlestone-dimension characterization (Kt0cP4MqKH).
  - LLMs gaming extensional verifiers in RLVR, with isomorphic perturbation testing (7thCbscUfa).
  - Adversarial hardening of rubric rewards (q27c1fw7O9).

  The 2026 counterpart attacks latent monitors with obfuscated activations (ktGmDGoWnB).
- **Multi-turn dynamics of refusal and sycophancy are a 2027 theme.** Sycophancy shows path dependence after pressure is withdrawn (5VV3kIBnXr). Crescendo histories shift refusal states (KyroQoSqjr). Other papers use phase-conditioned steering across clarify and refuse boundaries (ztEBx1jRPI) and intent-inferring dialogue critics (7xEAJ0wBYh). ReCrit penalizes capitulation to user criticism (7Pq65oEpUz).
- **Safety of sparse and new architectures.** Unsafe router paths in MoE LLMs appear in 2026 (JRtldw5Mpw). In 2027, multimodal MoEs show "safety write fragmentation" across modalities (GoRUW85YfE). Also in 2026: MASK-based jailbreaks of diffusion LLMs (jKQQb8uClw). Anti-distillation moves from reasoning traces served over APIs in 2026 (iuYd06fwPR) to open-weight models that reason in an encoded vocabulary in 2027 (lnQ8VcfPs5). Black-box forensics fingerprints the model behind an agent (vkYKZE0VXz, 2027).
- **Human authority and sociotechnical harms.** "Unauthorized decision closure" describes LLMs picking an option when the decision is reserved for people (5ZXeeljKSn, DecisionStateBench). CompulsionBench audits compulsion-risk proxies in RL-driven recommendation (wb8gzjfYu3). PrivacySkills shows agents choosing confidential sources unless given privacy guidance (7oN5Rjf8vo). All three are 2027. The 2026 papers in this batch are mostly classic DP, FL, membership-inference, backdoor and model-extraction work (Sr5KlyAnRG, 5ZSfNAvyzB, FlNRBVxwh6).
- **Recurring models and benchmarks, and methodological rigor.** The 2027 papers cite Qwen3/Qwen3.5 (4B/9B), Qwen3-VL, Qwen 3.7 Plus, OLMo-2, GPT-5.5, Opus-4.8 and DeepSeek-V4-Flash. Recurring benchmarks are AgentDojo, InjecAgent, Agent-SafetyBench, R-Judge, LIBERO (adversarial robustness of VLAs, M8x2pBFWET), JailbreakBench and Safety-Gymnasium. Several 2027 papers stress null controls and confound checks: DPO's recovery advantage disappears when answer options are reordered (YRQgASVpp3); directional alignment is not evidence of a shared mechanism (UxmHRGP8Xr); a pre-registered study of composed FL defenses (OEXuDVNvwu).
