# b38 observations (173 papers; 2026 vs 2027)

- **Agent authorization and memory are new 2027 threat surfaces.** The 2026 agent papers here are about injection and hijacking (TmJ4nj7W4K: MCP function hijacking, FunSecBench on BFCL). In 2027 the focus moves to the permission layer: authorization drift in dynamic policy systems such as Progent and DRIFT (eloUeXbRvL, AuthDiff), what an agent does after a tool call is denied (9hNkKIUgVu, DARP), memory-to-money authorization drift on autonomous payment rails (bMrVidVB7h, ghostpay benchmark), red-teaming OpenClaw-style skill and configuration files (jKaGpCYheS, SkillSafetyBench: 74% of the top 532 skills have at least one harmful configuration), and "task grafting" attacks on proactive agents that propose their own tasks (pZ0CYeNWhm).
- **Oversight is becoming theory and cryptography.** In 2027 there is a cluster of complexity-theory and cryptography papers on scalable oversight: debate with commitments and private communication (AGDWmpRmoE), multi-prover "rewinding" interactive proofs (jBlNOcywem), and zero-knowledge oversight of outputs computed from confidential data (PIsQv6iDXY). Applied control work also appears: weak-to-strong monitors given provenance and security hints (Yw5NmQ5VFd), audit horizons for coding agents with statistical risk control (JqWQsofwH0), and escalation from a weak to a strong computer-use agent with a shadow monitor (kDGgGKWYBi).
- **Deception and sandbagging research now uses model organisms.** 2026 work here builds benchmarks (jTHWqtQuDi: an honesty benchmark showing lying under pressure). 2027 work builds detectors and audits: honesty LoRA adapters trained on 533 model organisms and tested on AuditBench (gpt1K3Ia4D), and a paper showing that black-box sandbagging detection depends on the chosen reference condition (OwstUTHGOB). Both use pre-registration and held-out organisms.
- **2027 papers use more pre-registration and "the metric is wrong" audits.** Many 2027 papers are critiques of measurement rather than new methods: pooled AUROC for medical VLM report auditing is dominated by a no-image prior (1Ott6EBW32); object-hallucination benchmarks cannot certify per-object risk (GUXsvDPEru); KL divergence misranks prompt-injection robustness in diffusion LMs (HNkOvbXTb2); SAE causal claims depend on how they are measured (bbDKLX9z7A); attack order changes adversarial results (BKDuKefyJz); a theorem on when scalar metrics collapse (o2qnUZZlAv). Many of these mention preregistered splits, CIs and permutation tests, which is rare in the 2026 papers in this batch.
- **Reward hacking now targets judges and self-play.** In 2026 the reward-hacking work here is about reward-model robustness (oP99JQiDYp: causal rubrics). In 2027 it is about reference-free LLM judges being exploited in self-play: judge pass rate rises from 0.72 to 0.94 while true accuracy stays at 0.20 (Lpxn7lc6Dz). Related 2027 papers: dense CLIP rewards in diffusion RL do not transfer to external evaluators (kp05mLAe07), and math-trained PRMs are negatively aligned with information gain when used for scientific agents (4bP6tTSih9).
- **Jailbreak defense now reads the refusal trajectory.** Several 2027 papers track how harmfulness and refusal signals evolve across token positions rather than reading only the final token (qvVph9ziyG DualTrace, ZaUq6r82Eu HaRD, which also shows KV-compression defenses fail). Guardrails are moving toward policies specified in context at runtime (zIKFB5Tq85 SafePyramid, fq5ZeNtM9h AdaGuard). New modalities appear: audio-LLM steering (LxeR1hBLhw), embodied VLM planners whose refusals flip when one everyday object is added to the scene (MOmf9cjGQ2), and an omni-modal safety benchmark (SQ9jHvtFOh, 2026).
- **New societal-impact and privacy problems.** Measured market dilution by AI-written self-published books, 14k books with sales data through 2026 (KG6UT9fjNK). Web pretraining poisoning through comment sections, with up to 3.5% of Common Crawl documents affected (m2sITKrlDH). Developer behavioural fingerprints in coding-agent traces (SmdSMwBFQ3). Membership inference on in-context demonstrations (yYw1McdibB) and through recursive self-generation (gO6ADJNXMr). Keyed capability tiers inside a single set of open weights (pXCzwKrbrI).
- **Recurring models, benchmarks and tools.** In 2027, Qwen3 and Qwen3.5 are the default open models. Fictional or new model names appear, such as GPT-5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6, GPT-5.6-Luna and DeepSeek V4 Flash. Common benchmarks are AgentDojo, POPE/CHAIR/AMBER for hallucination, the RAID leaderboard for AI-text detection, BBQ and AuditBench. LVLM object-hallucination mitigation is still a high-volume, incremental area in both years (wpTLstf0kk, 6MnKb0RAu4, Xejin6zjRo, Y6s5gQi9lG).
